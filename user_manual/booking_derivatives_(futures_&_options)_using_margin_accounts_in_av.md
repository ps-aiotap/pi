Below are the steps to enter Futures transactions, so that only the
impact of MTM is correctly reflecting in the cash account:

**Booking Futures positions using a margin account**

**Step 1**

Create Asset : Payor account, for instance, "Futures Margin Account
xx1234" under **Menu** \> **Masters **\> **Accounts**.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5166666666666666in"}

**Step 2**

For any Buy or Sell transaction of futures, this account "Futures Margin
Account xx1234" must be selected in the Payee/Payor/Bank field.\
\
Go to **Menu **\> **Transactions** \> **Derivatives** to enter Buy or
Sell transactions.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in" height="3.575in"}

**Step 3**

Position MTM amounts which affect the Cash accounts should be separately
entered under\
**Menu **\> **Transactions** \> **Bank, Credit Card & Cash**. Choose the
Cash account in the "Account For" field. Select the respective
transaction type (Withdrawal for - ve MTM and Deposit for + ve MTM) and
date. Choose "Futures Margin Account xx1234" in the "Ledger Account"
field.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.4930555555555554in"}

On the Sell transaction, the actual Realized Gains are parked in the
"Other Income" account.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.8534722222222224in"}

The Futures Margin Account gets nullified once the position is unwound
(e.g. Sell transaction above).

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.827777777777778in"}
