Custodian Reconciliation will enable users to reconcile Investment
position\'s quantity, cost basis, market price, market valuation and
cash balance as on a particular date with a mapped direct Custodian
Feed.

**Finding Custodian Reconciliation**

**Menu Path: ***Transactions \> Reconciliation \> Custodian
Reconciliation*

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.661111111111111in"}

 

**Set up for Custodian Reconciliation**

Custodian reconciliation can be used for the accounts for which
Custodian feed holdings are available and feed account is mapped from
the Account Master. (See image below)

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.536111111111111in"}

User needs to select one or more custodian account(s) as on date, to run
Custodian Reconciliation. Only direct feed mapped custodian accounts
will appear in the filter selection for the selected Entity/Group. For
Group- All the mapped accounts for all the Entities of the group will
appear in the Account drop-down.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="1.4638888888888888in"}

- System will match the positions by security identifiers
  (ISIN/CUSIP/Symbol) and highlight the differences from the
  corresponding feed holdings

- Cash will be matched with default Payee/Payor/Bank account of
  Custodian account and any difference will be highlighted.

- Securities for which feed details are not matching or not available
  will have the text as 'No feed match' and the securities which are
  available in feed and not in system will be shown under 'Securities
  unmatched from feed'.
