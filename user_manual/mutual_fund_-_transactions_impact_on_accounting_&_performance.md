**Finding MF Transactions**

Path**:** **Menu **\> **Transactions **\> **Mutual Funds**

**Types of MF transactions & impact**\
\
**Purchase**\
**Use: **To enter the Purchase of Mutual Fund along with any
expenses/taxes paid.

**Reports Impact:** 

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **Net
  Amount is used as cashflow

- **General Ledger: **Custodian account is debited with \"Net Amount\".\
  \"Expenses & taxes\" amounts impact respective expense ledgers.
  However, expenses (like Brokerage, Stamp Duty and other charges)
  except STT can be capitalized through uploads and its impact can be
  seen on the Net Amount if 'Capital Expense' is checkbox is checked.\
  Bank is credited with total cash outflow.

**Negative Amounts supported: **

- Units - No.

- NAV per unit - No.

- Taxes & Charges - Yes.

 

**Switch-In**

**Use: **To enter the Switch In of Mutual Fund along with any
expenses/taxes paid.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **Net
  Amount is used as cashflow

- **General Ledger: **Custodian account is debited with \"Net Amount\".\
  \"Expenses & taxes\" amounts impact respective expense ledgers.
  However, expenses (like Brokerage, Stamp Duty and other charges)
  except STT can be capitalized through uploads and its impact can be
  seen on the Net Amount if 'Capital Expense' is checkbox is checked.\
  Bank is credited with total cash outflow.

**Negative Amounts supported:** 

- Units - No.

- NAV per unit - No.

- Taxes & Charges - Yes.

**Redemption**

**Use: **To enter Redemption of Mutual Fund along with any
expenses/taxes paid.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **Net
  Amount is used as cashflow

- **General Ledger: **Custodian account is credited with Cost.\
  Gain/loss will be booked.**\**
  \"Expenses & taxes\" amounts impact respective expense
  ledgers.However, expenses (like Brokerage, Stamp Duty and other
  charges) except STT can be capitalized through uploads and its impact
  can be seen on the Gross Amount if the 'Capital Expense' is checkbox
  is checked. Gain/Loss amount impacts the income account.\
  Bank is debited with total cash inflow.

**Negative Amounts supported: **

- Units - No.

- NAV per unit - No.

- Taxes & Charges - Yes.

**Switch-Out**

**Use: **To enter the Switch Out of Mutual Fund along with any
expenses/taxes paid.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I) : **Net
  Amount is used as cashflow.

- **General Ledger : **Custodian account is credited with Cost.\
  Gain/Loss will be booked.\
  \"Expenses & taxes\" amounts impact respective expense ledgers.
  However, expenses (like Brokerage, Stamp Duty and other charges)
  except STT can be capitalized through uploads and its impact can be
  seen on the Gross Amount if the 'Capital Expense' is checkbox is
  checked. Gain/Loss amount impacts the income account. Bank is debited
  with total cash inflow.

** Negative Amounts supported:**

- Units - No.

- NAV per unit - No.

- Taxes & Charges - Yes.

  \
**Dividend Reinvestment**

**Use: **To enter the Dividend Reinvestment of Mutual Fund along with
any expenses/taxes paid.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **No
  Impact.

- **General Ledger: **Custodian account is debited with \"Net Amount\".\
  \"Expenses & taxes\" amounts impact respective expense ledgers.\
  Income Account is credited.

**Negative Amounts supported:**

- Units - No.

- NAV per unit - No.

- Taxes & Charges - No. 

**Dividend Payout**

**Use: **To enter the Dividend Payout of Mutual Fund along with any
expenses/taxes paid.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **Net
  Amount is used as cashflow

- **General Ledger:** Bank account is debited with \"Gross Amount\".\
  \"Expenses & taxes\" amounts impact respective expense ledgers.\
  Income Account is credited with \"Net Amount\".

**  Negative Amounts supported: **

- Units - No.

- NAV per unit - No.

- Other Charges - Yes.

- TDS Amount - No. 

**Split**

**Use: **To enter the corporate action of Split.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **No
  Impact.

- **General Ledger: **No Impact.

**  Negative Amounts supported: **No.

 

**Consolidation**

**Use: **To enter the corporate action of Consolidation.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **No
  Impact.

- **General Ledger: **No Impact.

**  Negative Amounts supported:** No.

 

**Bonus**

**Use: **To enter the corporate action of Bonus Ratio.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **No
  Impact.

- **General Ledger: **No Impact.

**  Negative Amounts supported: **No.

 

**Merger**

**Use: **To enter the corporate action of Merger.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I):**\
  Transfer Amount is used as cashflow\
  Old security is transferred out at transfer amount\
  New security is transferred in at transfer amount

- **General Ledger: **Old Security :\
  Custodian account is credited with the Cost.\
  Transfer Clearing Ledger is debited.\
  New Security:\
  Custodian account is debited with the Cost.\
  Transfer Clearing Ledger is credited.

**  Negative Amounts supported: **No.

 

**Transfer-In**

**Use: **To enter the Transfer-In of Mutual Fund.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **Transfer
  Amount is used as cashflow

- **General Ledger: **Custodian account is debited with \"Net Amount\".\
  Transfer Clearing Ledger is credited with \"Net Amount\".

**  Negative Amounts supported: **

- Units - No.

- NAV per unit - No.**\**

 

**Transfer-Out**

**Use: **To enter the Transfer-Out of Mutual Fund.

**Reports Impact:**

- **Performance Reports (MPPR, PPR, WR, Cap. flow and CF-I): **Transfer
  Amount is used as cashflow

- **General Ledger: **Custodian account is credited with the Cost.\
  Transfer Clearing Ledger is debited.

**  Negative Amounts supported : **

- Units - No.

- NAV per unit - No.**\**

**Quick Notes**

*Dividend Payout should not be entered after full sell of a fund. It
should be entered before or on the full sell date to correctly capture
in performance.*
