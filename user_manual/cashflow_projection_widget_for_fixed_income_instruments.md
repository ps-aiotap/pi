The **Cashflow Projection Widget** allows you to forecast future cash
inflows from your Fixed Income instruments such as Bonds, FDs, CPs, and
more. By using real instrument data, the widget helps you plan liquidity
with accuracy and clarity.

This step-by-step guide will show you how to access, configure, and
interpret the widget on both **desktop and mobile**.

**1. What Does the Widget Do?**

The Cashflow Projection Widget:

- Projects future **interest payouts** and **maturity proceeds** for all
  eligible Fixed Income instruments.

- Shows cash inflows in clear **period buckets** (monthly, quarterly, or
  yearly).

- Displays both **cash inflow projections** and **cash balance
  trends** using intuitive charts and tables.

- Allows you to filter by entity, cash sources, and bank accounts for a
  customized view.

 

**2. The widget currently supports the following instruments:**

- Bonds / Debentures

- Corporate FDs

- Inter-Corporate Deposits (ICD)

- OFCD

- CP

- COD

- Fixed Deposits (FD)

**3. Accessing the Widget**

1.  Navigate to the **Report Book**

2.  Look for the **Cashflow Projection Widget**.

3.  Select whether you want to view the data as a **Table**, **Chart**,
    or both (Chart, Chart & Table combined view will be available soon).

**4. Configuring Your Report**

**Step 1: Set Report Date**

- Holdings are locked as of the **end-of-day** on the selected report
  date.

- Projections begin **from the next day** onwards.

**Step 2: Apply Filters**

You can refine your projection using the filters available:

1.  **Report Type:**

    - **Sequential :** Projects period-by-period (e.g., Month by Month).

2.  **Period:**

    - Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, or Financial Year (FY).

    - Choose **Number of Periods** (1--24).

3.  **Opening Cash Filters:**

    - **Primary:** Account (only Bank-tagged positions).

    - **Sub Group:** Select the specific bank accounts.

4.  **Sources of Cash:**

    - Currently available for **Fixed Income instruments** .

    - Sub-filters dynamically adjust based on your Source selection.

**5. Understanding the Projection Logic**

**Interest Payouts:**

- For FDs, CFDs, ICDs:

  - Calculated on **Principal**, **Interest
    Rate**, **Type** (Simple/Compound), and **Frequency**.

- For Bonds, CPs, and CODs:

  - Calculated using the **frequency master** defined for each
    instrument in their masters.

- Payouts are shown **in full** in the period they fall, not prorated.

**Principal / Maturity:**

- Redemption or maturity values appear in the **maturity month/year**.

- Zero-coupon bonds only show the principal at maturity.

**6. Viewing the Results**

After clicking **Process**, the widget displays the results in your
selected view:

**Table View **

- Opening Cash Balance (as of report date).

- Projected inflows from Fixed Income sources.

- Closing Balances for each period.

**Chart View (**Coming soon**)**

1.  **Projected Cash Flow (Bar Chart):**

    - Displays inflows (interest + maturity) per period.

2.  **Projected Cash Balance (Line Chart):**

    - Shows cumulative cash balance (Opening Balance + inflows).
