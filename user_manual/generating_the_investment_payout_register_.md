**\
About the Investment Payout Register**

This report gives a detailed view of the different payouts receivable
from various instruments like dividends, interest, rental income, and
more. 

Tip:  Reconcile your bank entries to highlight which of the receivables
were received or are yet to be received. 

**Finding the Investment Payout Register**

**Step 1 **

Go to 'Menu', 'Analytics', and then 'Investment Payout Register'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Generating the Investment Payout Register**

**Step 2**

Select your entity or group from the report filter, then choose the
report period along with specific dates and pick your report currency. 

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 3**

Finally, click \'Process\' to generate your report.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}
