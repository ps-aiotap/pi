*Morningstar (By All Accounts) is a feed provider that gathers,
transforms, and delivers financial account data specially from checking
credit cards, and bank accounts.*

**Activating your Morningstar account**

Except for the initial activation and sign-up, the account sync and
posting workflow is the same as all other AV feed providers. The
workflow is like Electra, PCR, except for some initial steps. This
document will cover the steps required to connect your account(s), map
them to the AV account, and sync and post transactions using the
Morningstar feed.

Note:  Connect with AV support to enable the Morningstar feed for your
AV Pro license if you haven't done so already.

**How to connect your account credentials to the AV system:**

**Step 1 **

Go to 'Menu,' 'Masters', 'Accounts', 'Connect Bank/Credit
Card/Investment Account', then 'Morningstar Connect'.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.365972222222222in"}

 

**Step 2 **

Select/Search the desired bank name, enter your credentials, and click
on "Connect". 

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.365972222222222in"}

**Step 3 **

The system will fetch all available accounts for the entered
credentials.

Select your account(s) and click on "Done".

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.365972222222222in"}

Once the accounts are synced, it takes around 30 minutes for the initial
data feeds to start flowing. The feeds support transactions up to the
past year.

**Step 4 **

Sync the account in the "Account Master".

Note:  This can be used to sync only checking and credit card accounts.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.3645833333333335in"}

**Step 5 **

Sync the transaction via "Transaction Sync". To do this, go to Menu \>
Transactions\> Transaction Sync.

![](media/image4.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 6 **

Process the transactions via the "Process" tab.

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.9965277777777777in"}

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.9965277777777777in"}

 

**Custom Account Mapping**

Custom Account Mapping is also available for Morningstar (BAA) feeds. It
helps users map ledgers based on the description updated by the feed
provider. 

For more help on using the Custom Account Mapping feature
click [here](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018469977-Custom-Account-Mapping-Auto-assign-feed-transactions-to-payee-payor-vendors-and-income-or-expense-accounts). 

 

**Bank Reconciliation**

You'll also be able to view your bank balance for synced bank accounts
from the feed in the "Bank Reconciliation" module. In this module, when
you click on the "Get from Feed" button, the system will fetch your bank
balance from Morningstar as on the specified date.   

Need help with bank reconciliation?
Click [here.](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/5758508421393-Bank-Reconciliation)
