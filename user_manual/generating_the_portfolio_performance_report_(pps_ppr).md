**What is the Portfolio Performance Report (PPS/PPR)?**

This is the performance report of your total wealth across a single
entity or a group of multiple entities.

**Generating the Portfolio Performance Report**

**Step 1 **

Go to 'Menu', 'Analytics', and then 'Portfolio Performance'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 2**

Choose appropriate filters to add to the pop-up dialogue box that opens,
to measure returns from inception and for a given period along with
Gains/Loss (realized & unrealized), dividend/interest/rental income, and
more. 

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}

**Step 3 **

Click 'Process' to view your report.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}

**Step 4**

Want to track advisor-wise performance for a chosen period? Simply
select "Advisor" in the secondary grouping!

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}
