**Your system's security is AV's utmost priority **

Passwords are crucial for security as they safeguard user
accounts. However, weak passwords may violate compliance standards.
To ensure data security and adhere to best practices,
follow AV's password policy guidelines. 

**How to log in to your account**

**Step 1 **

Log in to AV's system using your username and password. **\**

![Step](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.2916666666666665in"}

 

*Tip: Find the URL and username in the automated email sent to you upon
joining AV\'s platform. *

**What happens after 3 failed login attempts **

**Step 2 **

After 3 consecutive failed attempts, you'll get an email with password
reset instructions, and an on-screen message informing you of the same.

*Tip: Can't remember your login details? You can reset your password at
this point, instead of logging in multiple times,*

*by clicking the 'Forgot Password' link.*

![Step](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**What happens after 10 failed login attempts **

**Step 3 **

Fail 10 logins in a row, and you'll be locked out; an email sent to you
immediately thereafter describes how to restore your account. You can
connect to the AV support team to get back into the system or wait for
the system to automatically unlock after 30 minutes.

![Step](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

*Note: You'll also get one reminder email, 10 days before the password
expiry date.\*

- The admin user of this account is also notified via email.

- The \'Forgot Password\' link, disabled while your account is locked,
  can be used to reset your password after unlocking. This step is
  optional.

![Step](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

*Tip: Need to unlock the account immediately? Talk to AV support. *

**What happens after 15 failed login attempts **

**Step 4 **

If you\'ve continued to try and log in past the 10th attempt, upto 15
times, your IP will be locked. You\'ll have to contact the AV support
team for a resolution.

**Setting the password expiry frequency **

**Step 5 **

Admins can set password expiry from \'Systems\' in the \'Settings\' menu
-- the default is 60 days. Choose an expiry frequency and click \'Ok\'.

*Tip: This step is only for admins, so check your user access
permissions taking help from this article.*

![Step](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="1.9111111111111112in"}

**How password expiry alerts work **

**Step 6 **

System alerts to reset the password start 10 days before the password
expiry date (e.g., if the password expires on 30-Sep-2022, alerts begin
on 21-Sep-2022).

![Step](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

- Clicking \'Yes\' on the pop-up takes you to \'Settings\', then
  \'User\', and then \'Password Update\' to change the password.

- Click 'No' to login with the old password, which only works until the
  expiry date.

*Note: You'll also get one reminder email, 10 days before the password
expiry date.*

**How to reset your password **

![Step](media/image7.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 7 **

Clicking the 'Reset Password' button takes you to a new page, to update
your password. 

*Tip: Check the 'Password Guide' pop up on the left of your page for
help.* 

![Step](media/image8.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 8 **

To finish, click 'Update Password', then click 'Ok' on the pop-up.

![Step](media/image9.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5319444444444446in"}

**AV's password guidelines**

Creating a new password? Follow these guidelines for better security: 

**Your new password must:**

- be between 8 and 30 characters in length.

- contain at least one alphabet & number.

- contain at least one upper & lowercase character.

- contain at least one special character from !@#\$%\^&\*()+=

- contain no blank spaces between characters.

- be different than username, email id, or phone.

- not contain user's first or last name.

- not contain a sequence of repeated characters (Example: aaa123).

- not be the same as last 3 passwords.

*Tip: Avoid using your birth, anniversary, or other significant dates.*
