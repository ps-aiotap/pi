The all-new AV PRO Mobile App, which is fully synced with your AV PRO
license gives you seamless, on-the-go access to your latest financial
and investment reports -- anytime, anywhere. Here\'s a step-by-step
guide on how to download the app and begin using it to get an actionable
view of your wealth from the palm of your hand!  

**STEP 1 - Downloading the App**

Bring the power of Asset Vantage to your fingertips.

- For **iOS** users: Download from the [**Apple App
  Store**](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/av-pro/id6447855874)

 

- For **Android** users: Download from the [**Google Play
  Store**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.assetVantagePune.support)

** **

**STEP 2 - Logging In**

a.  **System Name**:

On the login screen, enter your System Name. This is case-sensitive and
derived from your platform domain. Example: If your domain is
"whitmore.assetvantage.com", the System Name will be "whitmore". Ensure
accurate spelling and capitalization to avoid login issues.

b.  **Username & Password**:

To log in, simply input your Username and Password exactly as you use
them for logging into the web version of your Asset Vantage system.

*Note: Both fields are case-sensitive, so it's crucial that you enter
the details precisely as provided to ensure smooth access*.

c.  **Face ID Setup**:

On your first login, you will be prompted to enable Face ID. This
feature provides a faster, secure login for subsequent sessions. You can
opt to enable or skip this feature at your discretion.

**STEP 3 - Navigating the Highlights Section**

Once logged in, you land on the **Highlights section** --- a centralized
dashboard featuring multiple **data widgets**.

**a. Widget Carousel**

The AV PRO Mobile App uses a swipe-able **carousel** to allow you to
swipe left or right to view different widgets.

- The carousel includes the following widgets: Net worth, Performance,
  Cash Balance, Income, and Expenses.

- You can easily navigate between widgets by swiping right or left
  horizontally.

- You can click on the individual bars for each widget to gain a
  drilldown view.

- For e.g.- If you click on the bar representing April in the Net Worth
  widget, you will be able to view a host of financial metrics basis the
  last date of that month. Selecting a different bar will reflect data
  for that particular month.\

**STEP 4 - Universal Filter For The Widget Carousel**

Located below the username, the **Universal Filter** allows you to apply
a combination of the filters you select to all the widgets,
simultaneously updating all of them. To view financial metrics for a
particular date and entity/group, you can select them in the universal
filter.

**STEP 5 - Individual Widget Filters For The Carousel**

If you want to apply a specific filter to an individual widget in the
carousel, tap the **filter icon
(**![](media/image4.jpeg){width="0.1527777777777778in"
height="0.16666666666666666in"}**)** on the top right corner of the
widget. This allows you to change the data view of that widget without
impacting other widgets in the dashboard. For Example: Click on 'Entity
& Group' and select 'Michael' if you want the selected widget to
represent the financial metrics for 'Michael'. Also, you can view data
for the parameter of your choice namely accounts, periods, currency,
asset class, advisor, geography, etc. by making edits to \'Primary
Grouping' filter. Similarly, you can play around with other filters to
customize your search.

 

![](media/image5.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"

**STEP 6 - Using the Burger Menu**

- The **Burger Menu** is in the top-left corner of the app (☰). Simply
  tap the icon to reveal a list of important widgets, and from there,
  you can easily navigate to any of the following sections:

  - **Home**

  - **Net Worth**

  - **Performance**

  - **Cash Balance**

  - **Income**

  - **Expense**

![](media/image6.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

**Let's understand each in detail:**

**1. Home: **Tap the Home button to return to the app's landing page,
where the 'Highlights' section is anchored at the top for instant access
to key financial insights.** **

**2. Net worth: **When you tap on Net Worth from the Burger Menu, you'll
see a bar chart showing your total net worth, clearly broken down by
asset class.

Tap any asset class (e.g., Real Estate) to view its detailed allocation
within your net worth. The app will zoom into the selected category and
display the corresponding value.

Tap on the individual bars to explore monthly trends for the selected
period. Each bar represents financial metrics as of the month-end date
across categories like Equity, Fixed Income, Alternatives, Real Estate,
Private Assets, Cash, Insurance, etc.

 

![](media/image7.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

 

**3. Performance:**

- When you select the Performance widget from the Burger Menu, the
  screen displays rectangular bars representing performance across MTD,
  QTD, CYTD, and FYTD. Below these bars, a list appears, showing each
  investment class ranked by market value. 

- When you select any investment class, the remaining classes will
  collapse, allowing you to isolate and drill down into that specific
  category. For example, if you tap on *Real Estate*, you\'ll first see
  the broader segment like *Real Estate Direct Property*, then drill
  further into individual investments such as *RAC Project Match Masters
  ABC and* finally access the market value tied to that specific
  investment. 

- You can refine the drill-down list of investment class performance
  using the filter menu with sorting options such as **High to Low, Low
  to High, Name (A--Z), and Name (Z--A).** 

- Tap the back arrow in the top-left corner to return to the landing
  page. \
   

![](media/image8.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

 

**4. Cash Balance:**

When you select the **Cash Balance** widget from the Burger Menu, a bar
chart appears, similar in layout to the Performance widget. Upon
clicking on **Cash**, a detailed flow-down will appear, displaying cash
balances across bank accounts, PMS, and other holdings for the selected
entity. You can then apply filter options to sort the cash balances
by **High to Low**, **Low to High**, **Name (A--Z)**, or **Name
(Z--A)**. 

Tapping on a bar reveals detailed cash balances for that month,
organized by the corresponding month-end date.

![](media/image9.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

** **

**5. Income:** When you select Income from the Burger Menu, a line chart
appears showing income data based on the date selected via the **date
picker **(accessible via the filter icon). Tapping any point on the line
or a bar reveals detailed income figures for each month-end. The chart
can be filtered using the same options---**High to Low, Low to High,
Name (A--Z), or Name (Z--A)**---to organize the data as needed. 

 

![](media/image10.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

 

**6. Expense:** When you select Expense from the Burger Menu, a line
chart appears showing expenses based on the date selected through the
date picker (accessible via the filter icon). Tapping any point on the
line or a bar reveals detailed expense data for each month-end. You can
sort the information using filters like **High to Low, Low to High, Name
(A--Z), or Name (Z--A)**.

**STEP 7 - Stealth Mode for Privacy**

Stealth Mode helps protect your privacy by masking absolute numbers when
the app is shared or viewed by others. Once activated, it conceals
absolute values and displays only high-level summaries.

- To activate Stealth Mode, tap the lock icon in the top-right corner of
  the screen.

- To deactivate Stealth Mode, tap the lock icon again to restore full
  visibility of your financial data.

**STEP 8 - Adding Widgets to Favorites**

a.  **Quick Add**:

- To add a widget to the Favorites section, click on the expand arrow on
  the widget. This will open a single-window view of that widget. Next,
  tap the Bookmark icon located at the top-right corner. Once selected,
  the widget will be added to your **Favorites **section. Any additional
  widgets added as Favorites will appear below the previously added ones
  in a stacked format.

- This preserves the widget's current filter settings, allowing you to
  access it later without the need to reapply them.

  

![](media/image12.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

b.  **Adding New Widgets**:

- To add a new widget to the **Favorites** section of your dashboard,
  tap the **plus icon (+)** and choose the desired widget from the
  available list. 

- The newly added widget will automatically be added to
  your **Favorites** section for easy access.

![](media/image13.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

                                         

**STEP 9 - Editing and Organizing Favorites**\
 

a.  **Rename Widget**:

- To rename a widget in your **Favorites**, just click the pen-shaped
  icon next to the widget name. This allows you to assign a personalized
  name to a widget for easier identification---for example, renaming
  \"Net Worth\" to \"Whitmore Net Worth.\"\
   

![](media/image14.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

b.  **Delete Widget**:

- To remove a widget from **Favorites**, tap the three-dot menu and
  select **Delete**. 

- This action removes the widget from the **Favorites **section without
  changing any underlying data or reports. 

 

![](media/image15.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

c.  **Copy Widget:**

To copy a widget, tap the three-dot menu and select **Copy**. This
creates a duplicate in the Favorites section, allowing you to explore
different filter combinations without changing the widget's original
configuration.\
\
       

![](media/image16.jpeg){width="6.263888888888889in"
height="3.0694444444444446in"}

d.  **Drag & Drop to Rearrange**:

- Favorite widgets can be rearranged by dragging and dropping them into
  the desired sequence. 

- This enables easy organization of widgets based on preferred order for
  quicker access. 

**STEP 10 - Universal Date Picker for Favorites**

The **Universal Date Picker** allows you to view the same financial data
across all widgets in the **Favorites** section at once.

- For example, you can select any month and year to see data for that
  specific date across all your widgets.

**STEP 11 - Updating Filters in Favorites**

Any changes you make to the filters within a widget in
the **Favorites **section will be automatically saved for that widget.

For example, if you update the date or primary grouping in a widget, the
app will retain those settings for that widget the next time you open
it.\
\
 

**STEP 12 - Logging Out**

To log out of the app, navigate to the **Burger Menu (☰)** and
click **Log Out**.

This will sign you out of the app, ensuring that your session has
securely ended.

**Additional Functionalities for the AV PRO Mobile App**\
 

**1.  Collapse or Expand the Carousel Highlights**

To hide the carousel, you can click the arrow next to your user name.
This will collapse the carousel and show only your Favorites on the
screen. 

*Note: You can expand the carousel again anytime by clicking the arrow
once more. *

**2.   Drill Down into Widgets**

You can drill down into each widget in the **Favorites **section to view
more detailed data. For example, selecting the Performance or Cash
Balance widget lets you drill down to specific assets or the entire
portfolio. You can further drill into any bar or chart to see an even
more detailed breakdown. 

*Note: The same functionality available in the Carousel section is also
accessible in the **Favorites **section. All permissible actions in the
Carousel---such as drilldowns, filter applications, and detailed
views---can also be performed on the widgets added to **Favorites**.*

**3.** **Configuring each widget**

You can select a personalized filter combination for any widget based on
your preferences and save that exact setup to the **Favorites **section
by tapping the bookmark icon. You can also create a tailored widget
directly in the **Favorites **section by tapping the plus
icon **(+)** and selecting from the available options. 

** **

**4. Updating Filters**

If you update any filters within a widget in the **Favorites **section,
the new settings will be automatically saved to your **Favorites**. For
instance, changes to the date or primary grouping will be remembered the
next time you open the widget. Any changes made to the widgets in the
carousel section will be automatically saved to the carousel section.
