**1. About Cashflow Projection Widget**

The Cashflow Projection Widget is a powerful tool that helps you plan
your finances by projecting your future cashflows over different time
periods. With this widget, you can easily evaluate your current
financial status, anticipate upcoming liabilities, and manage your
cashflows more effectively. You can use it to project your fixed-income
instruments available in the AV System, such as Bonds, Debentures, FDs,
PPF, CFDs, CODs, CPs, OFCDs, Inter-Corporate Deposits, and more. \
\
**2. Setting Up the Cashflow Projection Widget**\
\
**Step 1**  

**Setting up your Cashflow Projection Widget** 

Drag and drop the Cashflow Projection Widget onto your dashboard. Click
the filter icon to customize the data on your screen. \
 

**Step 2**\
** **

**Filters and Fields:** 

The Cashflow Projection Widget allows you to visualize data in     both
Table and Chart formats.      

Follow these steps to configure it: 

 

**Select Table or Chart View:**

**a. Table view** 

 

If you choose Table, the next field will automatically update to Table
Format. By default, the dropdown will be set to Horizontal, but you can
switch to Vertical if required. Choose Horizontal for a standard
left-to-right layout and Vertical for a top-down arrangement of your
data. \
 \
**b. Chart view**

 

When you select Chart, the next field will automatically update to show
Chart Type. From the dropdown, you can choose from various chart types,
including Area, Column, Line, Step Line, Line and Clustered Column,
Stacked Area, Stacked Column, and Heat Map, to dynamically visualize the
projected cashflows. 

**Select the Report Type**\
 

Specify the type of report you want for your projection. By default, it
will be set to \"Cashflow Projection". 

**Select the Entity**

  

Select the entity for which you want to project Cashflows. 

**Select the Period**  

Choose between monthly, quarterly, or yearly projections. 

**Select the Number of Periods** 

Define how many future periods (e.g., 12 months, 10 years) you would
like to project. If the coupon frequency is monthly but the selected
period is yearly, projected interest amounts will appear in the
respective months they are received. If the selected period aggregates
smaller intervals (e.g., yearly projection with monthly coupons), the
system will sum all payments for the year and display the total in the
respective column. 

**Select As on Date**  

Anchor your projections using a specific date. 

**Select Currency**  

Select your preferred currency to view the projections. \

**Step 3**\
\
**Generating your Cashflow Projection**

After selecting the appropriate filters, click the Process button to
generate the projection. The widget will display the projected cashflows
based on your selected parameters.

**3. Additional Widget Customizations to enhance your Cashflow
