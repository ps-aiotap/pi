**What is the Multiple--period widget? **

The multi-period chart enhances the analysis and visualization of time
series data. It provides a comprehensive way of comparing data across
different periods (multiple months, quarters, or years) while
emphasizing significant changes within the data. Through its ability to
illustrate the relationship between various data points, the
multi-period chart effectively reveals and conveys important trends and
patterns. 

**Checking your User Access Profile **

**Step 1 **

Ensure your user access profile can access the 'Report Book' in order to
add widgets to your dashboards.

*For detailed information about 'User Access Profiles', please refer
to  '[Setting up users & assigning user profile
permissions](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018470337-Setting-up-users-to-access-the-system-with-their-own-login-credentials-to-assign-user-level-profile-permissions-Admin-Read-View-Edit-Delete-etc-)'.*

**Navigating to the report book**

**Step 2**

To see your widget, go to 'Report Book' from the 'Menu' and find
'Multiple - Period\' in your widget library.

*For detailed information about the report book, please refer
to '*[Creating AV report
book](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403260437393-The-AV-Report-Book-explained)*'.*

**Setting up the Multiple - Period widget ** 

**Step 3**

Once you\'ve dragged and dropped the Multiple - Period widget onto your
dashboard, click the filter icon to filter the data on your screen.

- **Table/Chart: **See your data in either table or chart format

- **Chart Type:** Select various types of charts, each with its own
  strengths and purposes for better data visualization. 

<!-- -->

- Chart type options available here are :\
  Area\
  Column\
  Line\
  Step Line\
  Line and Clustered Column\
  Stacked Area\
  Stacked Column\
  Heat Map

<!-- -->

- You can select Inception IRR%, Inception BM IRR%, Period IRR%, Period
  BM IRR%, Inception TWR% (A), Inception BM TWR%, Period TWR%, Period BM
  TWR% from chart type series only  for  'Line', 'Column', 'Stacked
  Column' and 'Line and Clustered Column' chart type.

- **Area Series: **Choose up to four options in series for chart type
  Area, Line and Clustered Column, Column, Line and Step Line, but only
  1 option for Stacked Area and Stacked Column, as the system will
  show a break up for the tag selected in the Primary grouping. 

*Note - 'Area' in 'Chart Type' and 'Market Value' in 'Area Series' have
been selected by default; you'll need to manually select other data
points to process and view your data.*

- **Primary Grouping, Investment Universe Filter, Sub Filter:** This
  works the same way as explained in [Investment Policy Statement
  (IPS)](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/8141932939293-Investment-Policy-Statement-IPS-). 

- **Periods, Number of Periods, As On:** Based on the 'As On' date
  selected, data of periods will be computed in reverse order for the
  number of periods. For example, if you choose April 30,
  2023, as the 'As On' date and a 3-month time series, the report will
  show data for February, March, and April 2023. 

- **Order By: **Customize your data visualization order; display the
  most recent data at the beginning or at the end, depending on your
  specific analysis and presentation requirements. 

You can further configure the widget using 3 dots options

- **Show Currency Symbol:** This allows you to see data with its
  currency symbol.

- **Show Footer Notes:** This allows you to see notes related to the
  report at the bottom of the widget.

- **Show Chart Label:** This allows you to see the chart label on your
  chart report. This is applicable only for chart type reports.

- **Show Data Labels:** This allows you to see the labels of the data or
  the data values on the chart. For Multiple period widget you have
  option to see data Labels for the Open High Low Close, All and Only
  for Close values.

- **Show Legend:** This allows you to see the data legend for the chart.
  You have further option to display the legend at the Bottom, Left and
  Right of the widget.

- **Show Markers:** If selected, the series will show markers (circle,
  square, diamond, triangle etc.) on the charts to distinguish the
  series and the same can be printed.

*Note: \'Show markers\' are applicable on Area, Line, Step Line, Stacked
Area, and Line of Line and Clustered Column chart types.*
