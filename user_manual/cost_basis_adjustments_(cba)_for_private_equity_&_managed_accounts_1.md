**All about Cost Basis Adjustments (CBA)**

Cost Basis Adjustment (CBA) allows you to increase or decrease the tax
basis of private equity funds and managed accounts, which is then
recalculated based on the CBA entered. This article explains how to use
this feature on the AV system, and the impact it will have on your
reports and investments.

*Learn more about private equity
reports [here](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/search?utf8=%E2%9C%93&query=private+equity).*

**How to post CBA transactions**

**Step 1 **

Access 'Private Equity Funds' or 'Managed Accounts' by clicking
\'Menu\', then \'Transactions\', then add new entries by clicking the
\'+\' icon.

![step](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 2**

Choose \'Cost Basis Adjustment (CBA)\' from the dropdown list under the
\'Type\' column and select the account for which you want to post this
transaction.

*Note: The \'Ledger\' defaults to \'K-1 Income Account\', but can be
changed manually using the dropdown.*

![step](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.1631944444444446in"}

**Step 3 **

Enter the \'Adjustment Amount\' and the \'Adjusted Tax Basis\' will be
automatically computed after the CBA transaction. Use the \'Multi
Distribution\' icon to allocate a breakup of adjustments via
multi-distribution, across multiple income or expense accounts.

![step](media/image3.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 4 **

A voucher will be automatically created when you save the transaction.

*Tip: Click the voucher icon to the left of each transaction to view
respective vouchers.*

![step](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in" height="3.1375in"}

![step](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.1395833333333334in"}

*Did you know you could identify, analyze, and even rectify your voucher
entries? Learn how in the '[Voucher Audit
Report](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/5786883790481-Voucher-Audit-Report)'
article.*

**Impact of CBA on reports**

**Step 5 **

The position's running ledger balance will be updated as per the
adjustment amount and will reflect on General Ledger and Analytics
reports for Cost Basis. The \'Cost\' on reports will also show the
impact of CBA transactions posted.

*Note: \'Capital flow\' and \'Capital flow by Investment\' reports
remain unaffected by CBA adjustments.*

**Impact of CBA on private equity reports**

The PE report will include \'Cost Basis Adjustments\' and \'Tax Basis\'
columns. The former shows the sum of all adjustments from inception to
report date, while the latter reflects the running ledger balance of
securities, including CBA adjustments. The \'Cost Basis\' column
reflects the cost basis balance i.e., tax basis excluding CBA
adjustments.

![step](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.1458333333333335in"}

*Note: AV's system excludes Cost Basis Adjustment transactions while
computing auto-valuation. *

**Impact of CBA on performance numbers**

These investment positions with CBAs have no impact on performance
numbers like internal rate of return (IRR) or time-weighted rate of
return (TWR) since these are accounting/income adjustment entries and do
not represent actual cashflows.
