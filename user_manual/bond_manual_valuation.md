Have a Bond in Portfolio that has a different price than the one
available in feed? Now you can enter manual Valuation for listed Bonds.
Once enabled, you can enter valuations manually for listed bonds at the
account level. The system will use the entered valuations to compute
performance numbers and valuation. Continue to read for the steps to be
followed for the same.

**Enable manual Valuation from Position Master**

Go to **Menu** \> **Masters **\> **Positions**.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

![](media/image2.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

Once the Manual Valuation field is marked as Yes, the system will fetch
the Valuations posted in the system (like an unlisted bond). It will not
consider valuations available from the internal pricing database.

The system will give below pop-up message.

![](media/image3.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

If you click "Yes" in the pop-up message, the system will fetch the
historical month end valuations from the internal pricing database and
enter them as manual valuations in the system.

*Note: User can enable Manual Valuation for multiple positions using
multi-edit functionality.*

![](media/image4.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="1.8444444444444446in"}

If you click "No" in the pop-up message, the system will not fetch the
historical month end valuations from the internal pricing database.  For
historical dates, you will have to manually add Valuation transactions
in that case. Else, there is a possibility that performance reports may
show incorrect or different numbers than what you have seen previously.

*Note: If you change Valuation method back from manual to auto, the
system will fetch the valuations from the internal pricing database. The
manually entered valuations will not be considered by the system. The
valuations from the internal pricing database will be used on reports
and to compute performance numbers.*
