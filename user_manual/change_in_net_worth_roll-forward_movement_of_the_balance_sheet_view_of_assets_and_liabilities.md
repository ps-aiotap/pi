**About Change in Net Worth report**

Change in Net Worth report helps you to analyse the roll-forward
movement of the Balance Sheet view of Assets and Liabilities showing
opening value, contributions, distributions, net income, change in value
and closing balances grouped by position master tags over a custom
period. This includes all investment and non-investment ledger account's
movements.

This report brings the power of the Capital Flow by Investment into the
Balance Sheet using Primary and Secondary groupings.

The report supports partnership look-through and multi-currency.

**Finding the report**

**Step 1 **

- To generate this report from General Ledger, go to \'Menu\', head to
  \'General Ledger\', and select 'Change in Net Worth\'.

**Generating the report**

**Step 3 **

- The final processed report can be viewed or exported as per the user's
  access permissions. Reports can be exported in Excel or PDF format.
  The report can be generated in 'Grid view' as well along with Security
  Identifiers and position tags.

- All the Asset type accounts are grouped under 'Assets' grouping and
  similarly all the Liability type accounts are grouped under
  'Liability' grouping.

- Instead of the 'Total' row at the end of the report, there is 'Net
  Worth' row which reflects the net amount of 'Assets' minus
  'Liabilities' on opening and closing dates (first and last column).
  All the other columns will show the summation at Net worth row.

- This will enable to understand the change in net worth from opening
  date to closing date and see the flow of capital for all ledgers.
