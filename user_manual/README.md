## Conventions

- **Filenames** are slugged from the original titles (lowercase, underscores). Some guides exist in more than one revision (suffix `_1`, and similar); treat both as candidates and prefer the newer business process if they disagree.

- **Images** in the body often reference `media/` paths from the export. If those assets are not present next to a file, rely on the written steps and menu paths; screenshots may be missing locally.

- **Typos in filenames** (for example `general_legder`) match the on-disk name—use the link below, not the corrected spelling.

## Finding a topic

- Use your editor or ripgrep from the repo root, for example: `rg -l "wash sale" pi/user_manual`.

- Browse the **index by theme** below, or search by **`Module`** / feature keywords from the PI and open the closest guide.
