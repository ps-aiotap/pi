For Indian equities, stocks may be listed on the NSE, BSE, or both
exchanges. By default, the system will display NSE prices in the report.
However, if you need to view the price of a stock on a specific exchange
(NSE or BSE), you can select the desired exchange through the Position
Master by following these steps:

 

 

**Position in Asset Vantage**

 

- Represents an investment holding in an account.

- Example: If you hold Larsen & Toubro Ltd. stock in the \'Citi Bank\'
  Custodian Account and in the \'Kotak Securities\' Custodian Account,
  these are considered two separate positions in Asset Vantage.

- Positions cannot be manually created or deleted in Asset Vantage.

- Adding investment transactions to a custodian account will
  automatically create a position in the Position Master. 

- If the position already exists, new transactions for the same security
  will be added to that position.

- The Position Master is used to select and modify the default tagging
  of each position to organize reports accordingly.
