**Direct Equity** feature under Transactions helps you to post any
transactions which are related to the Equities/Stocks. One can post the
transactions by either manually adding the transactions or uploading a
bulk of transactions with the help of **\"Uploads\"** feature.

**Creating Accounts Master**

**Step 1**

Create Payor Account and Custodian Account from Accounts Master
([Masters Account
module](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018602817-Creating-masters-for-advisor-credit-rating-position-derivatives-future-options-Equities-stocks-securities-using-bulk-file-upload)).

**Adding Transactions**

**Step 2**

You can now add the Direct Equity transactions by clicking "+" sign
available in **Menu** \> **Transactions** \>\
**Direct Equity**. Save the entry by clicking on the "Save" icon.
