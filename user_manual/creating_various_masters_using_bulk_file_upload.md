**Master Creation**

**Advisor : Individual upload**

Advisor Master gives you the freedom to add a comprehensive list of all
your advisors for easy tagging and reference for existing or future
transaction uploads. This will also help in generating an advisor-wise
performance report.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.6145833333333335in"}

- Head to **Menu \> Masters \> Advisor** (under investments Master). You
  can either see the list of existing advisors by clicking on
  "Go" ![](media/image2.jpeg){width="0.4236111111111111in"
  height="0.3541666666666667in"}or you can start adding advisors by
  clicking on the "+" sign on the top right corner.

- After clicking on the "+" button, an advisor Add/Edit dialogue box
  will open. You can now add the Advisor name along with their code and
  then hit on **"Save"**.

![](media/image3.png){width="5.208333333333333in"
height="1.1458333333333333in"}

- You can view the added advisors list by clicking
  on **"Go" **![](media/image2.jpeg){width="0.4236111111111111in"
  height="0.3541666666666667in"}.** **You can also edit them later from
  the same screen by clicking on the **"Edit" **button.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.5347222222222223in"}

**Credit Ratings**

Credit ratings master shows the list of various credit rating agencies
with the ratings and their credit worthiness. This master allows you to
keep a consolidated master of all rating agencies. 

![](media/image9.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.5993055555555555in"}

- Head to **Menu \> Masters \> Credit Ratings**.** **

- You can upload new rating agencies and their credit worthiness by
  clicking on the "+" button on the top right corner. 

- An Add/edit dialogue box will open. 

- Enter the required fields and hit on **"Save"**.** **

- You can then view all the rating agencies by hitting on **"Go"**.** **

**Derivatives**

![](media/image11.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.595833333333333in"}

- Head to Menu \> Masters \> Derivatives (under Investments Master). You
  can either see the list of existing derivatives by clicking on "Go" or
  you can start adding by clicking on the "+" sign on the top right
  corner.

- After clicking on the "+" button, a Derivative Master Add/Edit
  dialogue box will open.

- You can add Derivatives Type, Instrument Type, Instrument name, Market
  Lot multiplier and Symbol

- The Master will automatically select your base currency as the default
  currency for the purpose of this master.

- The options in the drop down for \"Underlying\" will depend on the
  instrument type selected by you earlier.

- Also, strike price and expiry date needs to be fed, which will create
  a generic symbol for the Derivative Security for easy identification
  while making a transaction.

- You can now add the Advisor name along with their code and then hit
  on **"Save"**.** **

![](media/image12.png){width="5.618055555555555in"
height="2.138888888888889in"}

- You can also upload in bulk by using the **"AV format" **file.

- Head to **Uploads \> Sample AV Upload File**.** **Download the file
  and enter the details

- The upload process remains the same as explained in earlier modules
  \[Ref: Accounts : Bulk Upload\].

**Equities**

- Head to **Menu \> Masters \> Equities **(under Investments Master).
  You can either see the list of
  existing **"Listed" **and **"unlisted" **stock master by clicking on
  "Go" or you can start adding unlisted stocks by clicking on the "+"
  sign on the top right corner.

- After clicking on the "+" button, an Unlisted Equity Add/Edit dialogue
  box will open.

- Enter the Stock Name, Sector and Currency and then hit
  on **"Save"**.** **

![](media/image14.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="1.1201388888888888in"}

- You can also upload in bulk by using the **"AV format" **file.

- Head to Uploads \> **"Sample AV Upload File"**.** **Download the file
  and enter the details. 

- The upload process remains the same as explained in earlier modules
  \[Ref: Accounts : Bulk Upload\]. 

- You can view the list of all **"Listed" Stocks or "Unlisted" **Stocks
  on the same screen. 

- Select one of the options between "Listed" and "Unlisted" and then hit
  on Go.
