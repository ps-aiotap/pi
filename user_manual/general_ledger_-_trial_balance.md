**About the Trial Balance**

A trial balance is a bookkeeping worksheet in which the balances of all
ledgers are compiled into debit and credit account columns, the totals
of which are equal (in line with the double entry bookkeeping system).
The general purpose of producing a trial balance is to ensure entries in
the bookkeeping system are mathematically correct. 

**Finding the Trial Balance**

**Step 1 **

Go to 'Menu', then 'General Ledger', and finally, 'Trial Balance'.

**Generating the Trial Balance**

**Selecting appropriate filters**

**Step 2 **

Check and select each filter from the top right corner of the page, as
per your requirement, to generate the trial balance.

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

- **Template:** The 'Template' dropdown has two predefined templates -
  US GAAP and IFRS. However, if you need to configure custom templates,
  you can do so by going into 'Masters' from the 'Menu,' and then
  selecting 'Chart of Accounts'.  *For detailed information about this
  process, please refer to [this
  article](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018745738-Setting-up-and-customizing-chart-of-accounts-COA-using-the-base-GAAP-and-IFRS-templates).*

- 

**Report Period:** This dropdown shows you all the financial years since
inception. You can also run this report for custom dates by selecting
the 'From' and 'To' options.

- 

**Process:** Click 'Process' to run the trial balance report.

**Processing the Trial Balance**

**Step 3 **

By default, this report is processed and viewed in the \'Summary\' view.
To see account level details, you can expand specific accounting groups,
or alternatively, click on the \'Expand All\' or \'Collapse All\' icons.

At this level, you can right-click specific accounts and select \'Show
Ledger\' to view ledger balances and journal entries. Alternatively, you
can make changes via the 'Edit' button.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.3777777777777778in"}

**Exporting the Trial Balance**

**Step 4 **

The final processed report can be viewed or exported as per the user's
access. Exported reports will be available in Excel or PDF format.

height="3.1881944444444446in"}
