**About the Income Statement**

The income statement is an entity\'s financial statement and reflects
the income, expenditure, and profit/loss for a given period. The profit
or loss is determined by taking all revenues and subtracting all
expenses from both operating and non-operating activities. 

**Finding the Income Statement **

**Step 1 **

Go to 'Menu', then 'General Ledger', and finally, 'Income Statement'.

**Generating the Income Statement **

**Step 2 **

**Selecting appropriate filters **

Check and select each filter from the top right corner of this page, as
per your requirement, to generate the income statement.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.467361111111111in"}

- **Template**: The 'Template' dropdown has two predefined templates -
  US GAAP and IFRS. However, if you need to configure custom templates,
  you can do so by going into 'Masters' from the 'Menu,' and then
  selecting 'Chart of Accounts'. *For detailed information about this
  process, please refer to [this
  article](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018745738-Setting-up-and-customizing-chart-of-accounts-COA-using-the-base-GAAP-and-IFRS-templates).*

- **Report Period:** This dropdown shows you all the financial years
  since inception. You can also run this report for custom dates by
  selecting the 'From' and 'To' options.

- **Denomination:** This field lets you run this report in thousands,
  lacs, etc.

- **Process:** Click 'Process' to run the income statement.

**Exporting the Income Statement**

**Step 3 **

The final processed report can be viewed or exported as per your access.
Exported reports will be available in Excel or PDF format.
