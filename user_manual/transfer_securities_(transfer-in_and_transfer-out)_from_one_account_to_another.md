Transfer of Securities means transferring the securities from one
account to another. This process applies to Direct Equity, Mutual Fund &
Fixed income. In this module we will take an example of Direct Equity.

**Posting Transfer-Out Transaction**

**Step 1**

Go to **Menu **\> **Transactions **\> **Direct Equity**.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.033333333333333in"}

**Step 2**

- Click on the "+" sign on the top right corner.

- Place a Transfer-Out transaction with required details i.e. Investment
  Account, Security Name,\
  Units, Price, etc. Note that a default Payee/ Payor/ Bank account will
  be automatically tagged as\
  'Transfer Clearing a/c'.

- You can also change to any other desired Payee/ Payor/ Bank a/c.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.9194444444444443in"}

- Enter the Transfer Date (which is the date on which you want to
  transfer the securities on).\
  This will be considered to show the positions in books/reports.

- Select the Security Name and enter the number of Units you want to
  transfer.

- Set the Transfer Price and hit Save.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.9194444444444443in"}

*Note: The securities will be transferred out on original purchase cost
hence no capital gains will be*\
*applied. In case of multiple lots, system will consider purchase cost
of each lot. You just need to enter*\
*the no. of units to be transferred.*

- The transfer-out transaction gets posted.

- You can check the voucher details to see the accounts being
  transferred.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0208333333333335in"}

**Posting Transfer-In Transaction**

There are two ways to do this:

- Manual entry (advisable for single transfer ins).

- Using the "Transfer In Download" ready excel file ( advisable for
  multiple transfer-ins).

1\. For "manual entry",

- Click on the "+" sign to book a transfer-In entry.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.407638888888889in"}

- Enter the required fields.

- Transaction date - The date of original purchase (this will be
  considered to compute holding period for gains).

- Price is the original purchase price/cost of the security. (Accounting
  entry, Wealth Register,\
  Balance Sheet & Gains Report will consider this price for generating
  the report).

- Transfer Price is used for performance calculation and does not have
  any impact on accounting entries.

- Transfer Date is the date of transfer of securities; this will be
  considered to show the positions in\
  books/reports.

2\. An alternative way to do this, is to use the ready download feature
which lets you download the transfer in entries in the AV excel format
and upload directly to post Transfer-In entries. This saves up time for
multiple transfer-Ins.

- Check the box against the transfer out entry.

- Click on the download " Transfer-In Excel " from top right corner.

- This will download a ready excel file in AV upload format which you
  can use to upload directly for all transfer in entries.

- All you need to do is enter the Custodian Name and Account Number in
  the downloaded excel file.

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"

*Note:For out of turn transactions of Transfer-In / Transfer-Out
transactions, system will notify you to correct those transactions
before proceeding further. In case of Fixed Income, an additional field
(Accrued Interest which has to be per unit instead of total amount) is
also available if you wish to transfer that too.*

**Impacts on Analytics**

**Wealth Register**\
Will show the position in report as per Transfer Date with original
cost.

**Portfolio Performance**\
Will show the position in report as per Transfer Date however it will
consider Transfer Price for performance computation.

**Balance Sheet / Trial Balance**\
Will show the position in report as per Transfer Date with original
cost.

**Gains report**\
Will show the position as per Transfer Date and original cost however
days to long term will be considered with original transaction date.
