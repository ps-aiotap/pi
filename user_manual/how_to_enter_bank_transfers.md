Bank Transfers happen when you transfer funds from one bank account to
another one.

Want to post such bank transfer transactions in your AV system? Follow
the below steps to know more.

There are two methods to book bank transfer transactions:

**Method 1**

The first way to enter a bank transfer is by entering the transaction in
both accounts.

**Step 1**\
\
**Menu **\> **Transactions **\> **Bank Credit Card Cash**.

**Step 2**

Select the entity and the account from the bank account that the money
is coming out of/and or going into.

**Step 3**

Enter a withdrawal transaction from the bank account which the funds
were transferred out of. In the ledger account field, use the transfer
clearing account.

![](media/image2.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.911111111111111in"}

**Step 4**\
\
Then enter a deposit transaction from the account where the funds were
transferred into. In the ledger account field, use the same transfer
clearing account that was used in the previous withdrawal transaction.

![](media/image3.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.017361111111111in"}

![](media/image4.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.826388888888889in"}

*Note: In both of these transactions you must select the same transfer
clearing account in the ledger account, for it to be zeroed out. *

**Step 5**\
\
This is the preferred method for recording bank transfers. The benefit
of this method is that in the transaction list view, the transaction
shows up in both accounts (as a withdrawal in one account and a deposit
in the other).

Also, if you are using the bank's data feeds for both accounts, it will
show similar withdrawal and deposit transactions in the feed as well.
There is a risk of duplication if you process both transactions directly
in the other bank account without using the intermediate Transfer
Clearing account.

*Tips: You can use the notes or description fields to add details about
the bank transfer.*

**Method 2**

The second method to enter a bank transfer is by entering a transaction
in only one account.

**Step 1**\
\
**Menu **\> **Transactions **\> **Bank Credit Card Cash**.

**Step 2**

Select the entity and account in the drop-down menu where the money is
going out of/and or into.

**Step 3**

You can enter a withdrawal (or deposit) in one bank account and select
the second account in the "ledger account" field. In this method, there
is no need to select intermediate (transfer clearing account).

![](media/image7.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0770833333333334in"}

![](media/image8.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"

**Step 4**

The benefit of this method is you do not need to enter two transactions
in the system. However, in the transaction list view, the transaction
shows up only in the account in which it was originated from (it does
not show up in the transaction list view of the account selected in
"Ledger Account" field.

However, if you look into each bank's general ledger view, the complete
journal entry the Cr and Dr shows up correctly.
