Looking to streamline how you classify individuals in your system? A new
'Non-Resident' checkbox has been introduced specifically for Individual
entity types. With a single click, you can now mark an entity as
non-resident - making identification easier and cleaner, without
impacting taxation or other system functionalities.

  **Step 1: Where to Find the \'Non-Resident\' Checkbox**?

- Go to **Menu **\> **Master **\> **Entity**

- Open the Add, Edit, or Copy screen for an Individual entity

- You\'ll find the \'Non-Resident\' checkbox right after the 'Entity
  Type' field

**Step 2: Marking an Entity as Non-Resident**

- Check the \'Non-Resident\' box while creating, copying or editing an
  entity

- The entity will be flagged as Non-Resident

**Step 3: Keeping an Entity as Resident (Default Setting)**

- If you leave the checkbox unchecked, the entity will remain a
  Resident---this is the default setting.

- The entity functions as a standard Individual entity

**Step 4: Entities Other than Individua**l

- For all other entity types, the \'Non-Resident\' column will remain
  blank.

- This option is available only for Individual entity types, keeping
  your classification clean and relevant.

**Other Important Details -**

- This feature does not impact taxation or alter any other system
  calculations---it's purely for classification.

- The \'Non-Resident\' column will be included in Excel exports, so your
  reporting stays complete and consistent.

*This new feature ensures better classification of entities without
disrupting your existing workflows. It's a simple yet effective addition
that brings sharper visibility to your data. Be sure to update your
entities accordingly to maintain accuracy and improve clarity in your
reporting.*
