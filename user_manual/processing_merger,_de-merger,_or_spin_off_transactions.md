**About Merger, De-merger, or Spin Off Transactions**

Used in both Direct Equity and Mutual Funds, this process allows for
booking both merger and de-merger transactions for Direct Equity, while
you can book only merger transactions for Mutual Funds.

*Note: This article only focuses on Direct Equity, but the process
remains the same for both investment types.*

**Booking Merger Transactions**

**Step 1 **

Go to 'Menu', 'Transactions', then 'Direct Equity', then click on the
"+" sign on the top right corner of the page.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 2**

Add/edit enter all the required details. 

- **Entity:** Select your "Entity".

- **Type:** Select "Merger" from the dropdown.

- **Account:** Selecting an account will automatically update the "Payee
  / Payor / Bank" field as "Transfer Clearing".

- **Security Name:** Select your "Security Name"; this is the original
  security.

- **Security Name New:** This will be the new security which is merging
  into your feed in the Merger ratio; based on this, the "Units" will
  automatically be calculated.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.189583333333333in"}

**Step 3**

Click the "Save" icon.

**Step 4**

Once the transaction is saved, you can see the Merger entry passed and
the subsequent "transfer ins / outs" being created by the system.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in" height="2.71875in"}

**Booking de-merger transactions**

**Step 5 **

Follow Step 1 to go the de-merger transaction.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 6**

In the DE transaction, add/edit enter all required details.

- Select the "Entity" and then select "De-Merger" from the "Type" drop
  down list.

- Select the "Account". This will automatically update the "Payee /
  Payor / Bank" field as "Transfer Clearing".

- Select the "Security Name". This is the original security.

- "Security Name New" will be the new security which has de-merged into
  the feed in the De-Merger ratio; based on this, the "Units" will
  automatically be calculated.

- You have to enter the '% Cost of Acquisition' of the new de-merged
  security.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in" height="2.71875in"}

**Step 7 **

Click "Save".

*Note: Multiple de-merger transactions can also be passed by clicking
"+" in the 'Security Name New' field and following the same process as
mentioned in Steps 1 and 2. *

![](media/image5.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 8 **

Once the transaction is saved, you can see the de-merger entry passed
and the subsequent "transfer-Ins / outs" being created by the system.

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in" height="2.71875in"}

*Notes:\*

- * The original lots will be maintained even after merger / de-merger
  transactions.\*

- *The system will automatically create the corresponding transfer-in /
  out transactions. \*

- *The transfer out will be of the old stock with the old cost price and
  the transfer in will be of the new merged / de-merged stock on the
  basis of lots available.*

- *The transfer date will be of the date of merger / de-merger, and the
  transaction date will be on the original transaction date.*

**Final impact**

The merger/ de-merger positions will show on the **Wealth Register /
Portfolio Performance Report / Balance Sheet** from the date of the
merger / de-merger transactions.

**In the Gains Report, **the holding period for merger / de-merger
stocks will be considered from the original purchase transaction date.
