Bank accounts which are linked to custodian accounts (e.g. Brokerage
accounts) need to be linked with respective custodian account to
consider them as a combined account for Capital flow report.

*Note: Bank accounts which doesn't have any linked account will be
considered as Individual/Standalone bank account and Custodian accounts
which are not linked to any Bank account will be considered as
Individual/Standalone accounts.*

**Heading to Account Master**

Go to** Master \> Accounts \> Asset: Bank / Liability: Bank**.**\**

- User needs to mark the bank accounts which are linked to custodian
  account and there will be only one to one mapping.

- Dropdown values will be the list of custodian accounts for which
  default Payee/Payor/Bank is the selected bank account.
