**What is the Report Book?**

AV\'s report book allows you to custom configure a consolidated view of
your data on one or more interactive dashboards that can be combined
into a branded exportable PDF report book at any time.

Each dashboard can be built with multiple views from a library of
configurable widgets that can be resized and setup to show holdings,
performance, asset allocation and general ledger reports in tables or
charts as you like.

**Finding the Report Book **

**Step 1 **

To navigate to the report book, go to 'Menu', and then 'Report Book'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

*Note: Can\'t see it? Check your access permissions from the \'User
Access Permissions\' tab in the \'Settings\' section of the \'Menu\'.
Learn more from the \'[User Access
Permissions](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018470337-Setting-up-users-and-user-profile-permissions)\' article. \*

**Adding dashboards**

**Step 2 **

Click the '+' icon inside the \'Report Book\' to add a new dashboard.

*Note: For new users with no dashboards, an 'Add Dashboard' pop-up
appears automatically as soon as you enter the 'Report Book'. *

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.9118055555555555in"}

**Configuring dashboards **

**Step 3 **

Add details in each tab inside the pop-up dialogue box, to set
preferences for the color scheme, final print copy, and more. 

Learn how to set your preferences in
detail, [*here*](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/8104162677533). 

![](media/image3.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

**Step 4 **

Click \'save\' to save your changes in the new dashboard.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

**Step 5 **

Explore more configurable options from the settings menu in the top
right corner of your dashboard page.

*Note: These settings apply to all dashboards and will override
individual dashboard settings. \*

![](media/image5.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.3819444444444446in"}

Learn more about dashboard
configuration [*here*](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/8104162677533). 

Want to setup dashboard access for report book? To know more
click [*here*](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/24001256503193).

**Adding widgets **

**Step 6 **

Unlock your new dashboard to add a widget.

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.252083333333333in"}

**Step 7 **

Click on 'Add Widget' to head to the widget library. Choose between 'Pie
& Table', 'Bar & Table', and more, then drag your preferred widget into
the dashboard, where you can resize it as per your preference.

![](media/image7.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Explore our widgets: **

- [Full Page - Auto Expand
  Widget](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/7497697920797-Full-Page-Auto-Expand-Widget-)

- [Investment Policy
  Statement](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/8141932939293-Investment-Policy-Statement-IPS-)

- [Multiple Period
  Widget](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/18650417400089)

**Configuring widgets using widget settings** 

**Step 8 **

Click the filter icon inside each widget to explore and use widget
settings, then click 'Process' to view your reports. 

*Note: Widget settings are (mostly) different for different widgets. \*

![](media/image8.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

To explore how to efficiently configure widgets, including changing
their names or exporting them,
click [*here*](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/19873000978585). 

![](media/image9.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

**Printing your dashboard**

**Step 9 **

Click on the 'Print Dashboard' icon to generate a download request. A
PDF file is then automatically downloaded onto your device.

*Note: The print preferences will automatically be selected as per your
dashboard settings. \*

![](media/image10.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Printing your Report Book**

It's not just the dashboard that can be printed! You can print multiple
dashboards into one 'book', hence the name -- the Report Book.

**Step 10 **

Click the 'Report Book Group' icon.

**Step 11 **

Configure your report book before printing.

![](media/image11.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Add Report**

- Click '+' to add a report. The pop-up that appears will have
  'General', 'Branding', and 'Print Preferences\' tabs like in the
  dashboard settings mentioned in the '[*Configuring dashboards with
  dashboard
  settings*](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/8104162677533-Configuring-dashboards-with-dashboard-settings)' article.
   

- Use the \'Dashboards\' tab to create groups of dashboards, and
  rearrange them as necessary, for printing.

![](media/image12.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

- Click the 'Save' icon to save your changes.

**Edit, Delete, Go To Dashboard**

- Edit, delete, or return to the main page as needed.\
  ![](media/image13.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
  height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 12 **

Click the PDF icon to print your report book.

![](media/image14.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

single report book. You can see the total number of dashboards selected

Tip: To print more than 10 dashboards in one Report Book, simply create
more Report Book groups, a feature Pro and Prime users can use to an
unlimited extent. *
