Want to add home improvement transactions for your real estate
investments? Follow the below steps for the same.

**Path to Real Estate Transaction**

Menu Path: **Menu **\> **Transactions **\> **Real Estate**

**Enter Home Improvement Transaction**

**Step 1**

Click on "+" sign in the top right corner to add a transaction.\
\
![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.584722222222222in"}

*Tips: If there are prior transactions, you can select the "copy" button
and change the transaction type to \'Improvements\'.*

![](media/image2.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.495833333333333in"}

**Step 2**

Enter the amount of the improvement, the date it was done, and add
specific notes. Then when finished click save.

![Snip](media/image3.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.848611111111111in"}

 **Step 3**

To view the journal entry, click on the voucher/price tag icon. The
investment account will show the Dr and the cash/bank account will show
the Cr.

![Snip](media/image4.jpeg){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.8868055555555556in"}
