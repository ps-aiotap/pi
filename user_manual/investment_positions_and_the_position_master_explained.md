One of the most powerful features of AV is the Position Master and the
concept of the investment Position.

**Position - meaning**

Investment holding in an account.

- Example: AAPL stock held in Custodian Account xxx444 and AAPL stock
  held in Custodian Account xxx555 are two independent positions in AV.

- Positions cannot be created or deleted manually within AV. 

- Adding investment transactions to a custodian account automatically
  creates a Position in the position master. In case the position
  already exists, then new transactions for the same security will
  simply accumulate to that position.

- The position master is used to select and change default tagging of
  each position to group reports accordingly.
