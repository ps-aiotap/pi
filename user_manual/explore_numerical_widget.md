**About the Numerical widget**

Introducing a new Numerical Widget in the Report Book which will help
you to easily showcase your total portfolio worth along with its
performance percentage.

**Navigating to the report book**

**Step 1 **

To see your widget, go to 'Report Book' from the 'Menu' and find
'Numerical' in your widget library.\
For detailed information about the report book, please refer to
\'[Creating AV report
book](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403260437393-Creating-AV-report-book)\'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

Note: Can\'t see it? Check your access permissions from the \'User
Access Permissions\' tab in the \'Settings\' section of the \'Menu\'.
Learn more from the \'[User Access
Permissions](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018470337-Setting-up-users-and-user-profile-permissions)\'
article.

**Setting up the Numerical widget**

**Step 2 **

Once you\'ve dragged and dropped the 'Numerical' widget onto your
dashboard, click the filter icon to filter the data on your screen.

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

The filter options are similar to what we have throughout the system.

- **Report Type**: The preset report type for this widget is
  \"Numerical\".

- **Select Columns**: The default option provided under this filter is
  "Closing Value", which will provide closing values for the selected
  period.

- **Report Period**: This dropdown offers various choices, allowing you
  to easily access MTD, QTD, YTD, and multiple annual reports, or
  customize the reporting period by specifying start and end dates.

- **Other Fields**: Customize this widget to suit your preferences with
  features such as \'Valuation With Accrued Income\', \'Display
  Currency\', \'Show Change\', \'Include Partnership Positions\', and
  more.![](media/image3.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
  height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 3 **

After selecting appropriate filters and clicking the 'Process' button,
the widget will appear with data, as shown
below.![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.189583333333333in"}

Note:  You can explore more widget settings like Refresh, Filter, Column
Sorting, and more, to customize any number of widgets in your
dashboard(s). Learn more about configuring widgets using widget
settings, [here](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/19873000978585-Configuring-widgets-using-widget-settings). 

**Printing your dashboard**

**Step 4 **

Click on the 'Print Dashboard' icon to generate a download request. A
PDF file is then automatically downloaded onto your device.

![](media/image5.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

Tip: Remember to save your dashboard and its settings to be able to
print them with the right information.
