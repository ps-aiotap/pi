This option on the AV system lets you enter all transactions pertaining
to banks, credit cards, and cash.

**How to post these transactions**

**Step 1**

Go to 'Menu', then 'Transactions', and finally, click on 'Bank, Credit
Cards & Cash'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

*Note: There are three ways you can enter these transactions --
manually, via bulk upload, and through bank sync.*

**Posting transactions manually**

**Step 2**

To enter transactions manually, click the '+' sign on the top right
corner to open a pop-up box.

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

- **Entity:** Select an entity from the dropdown or add a new entity by
  clicking the '+' sign.

- **Type:** Choose between Deposit, Withdrawal, or Check, or hover over
  the information icon to know more.

- **Account For:** Select the bank account from the dropdown, the
  details of which you will have created in the 'Masters' section. For
  more information, check out Setting up master accounts: Assets,
  Liabilities, Equity, Income, and Expense. Or, add the account
  directly, using the '+' sign.

- **Payee/Payor/Vendor:** Use the dropdown to select payees, payors, or
  vendors.

*Note: This is not applicable to bank transactions.*

- **Transaction Date:** Select the transaction date.

- **Ledger Account:** Select the ledger account where you want the
  transaction to be posted.\
  \
  *Note: The 'Multi Distribution' icon can be used to add and distribute
  a single transaction into multi-ledger accounts. \*

![](media/image3.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

- **Amount:** Enter the amount of your transaction.

- **Related to Asset/Liability Position Tag:** Tag any other custodian
  account for reporting purposes, in the 'Investment Payout
  Register'. *Learn more about this in the '[Investment Payout Register:
  Dividend, Interest & Rental Income payouts across entities &
  accounts](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403231374097-Investment-Payout-Register-Dividend-Interest-Rental-Income-payouts-across-entities-accounts)' article.*

- **Status:** This is for the check print facility through the bank.
  Updating the status as \"R : Reconciled\" will mark the transaction as
  reconciled in the bank reconciliation module. *To know more, read our
  article on '[Bank
  reconciliation](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/5758508421393-Bank-Reconciliation#Reconciled_status_in_Bank_cash)'.*

- **Notes, Memo, Documents, Tags, and Check #:** Enter custom notes,
  memos, upload documents, search for tags, and check numbers via these
  fields.

**Uploading transactions in bulk**

**Step 3**

Enter transactions faster with this feature. Get started by clicking the
'Upload' tab and downloading the sample AV upload file.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}

**Step 4**

Enter your details in the downloaded Excel.

*Note: All columns correspond to form fields as seen in the manual entry
process in Step 2. *

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.189583333333333in"}

**Step 5**

Save the excel file, head over to the AV system, select your date
format, and click on 'Upload'.

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4194444444444443in"}

**Step 6**

The transactions will be uploaded on the list view screen and will
appear in green. 

*Note: Any \'red\' entries are errors and need to be resolved. Click the
edit icon against every red entry to fix errors, then*\
*save your changes.*

![](media/image7.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}

**Step 7**

Once fixed these, the entries will appear in 'Green'; then, select all
the transactions (from the dropdown), check the boxes, and then click
'Process'.

![](media/image8.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}

**Step 8**

Processed entries can be viewed in the 'Details' tab; click 'Details',
select the date range, and click 'Go' to see all recently uploaded
entries on your screen.

*\*

*Note: In the Excel, the transaction status can be uploaded as \"R :
Reconciled\" to auto-reconcile the transaction in the*

*bank reconciliation module. *

![](media/image9.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}
