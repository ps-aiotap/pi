Managing corporate actions like Dividend Payouts, Bonus
Issues, Consolidations, and Stock Splits can seem complicated, but with
this step-by-step guide, you'll be able to process them efficiently
using the AV system. 

***Note:** You can only process corporate actions if all relevant
transactions have been entered and positions are available in the
system.* 

**Finding Corporate Actions in the AV System **

To begin, navigate to the Corporate Actions section:** **

**Menu \> Transactions \> Corporate Actions **

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.3305555555555557in"}

**Filter Options for Corporate Actions: **

You can process corporate actions for single or multiple holdings across
all entities and groups simultaneously. To simplify your workflow:** **

- **Filter by Account, Holding Name, and Date Range \**
  Refine your data by applying these filters on the corporate actions
  screen.** **

<!-- -->

- **Set TDS Percentage \**
  This automatically calculates TDS on dividends based on your selected
  percentage.** **

**Running Corporate Actions for Single or Multiple Entities/Groups**

**Step 1. Select Entity/Groups **

** **Choose the entity or group for which you want to run the corporate
actions. 

**Step 2. Default Filter **

The second filter automatically defaults to "Equity" since corporate
actions are only applicable for Direct Equity.** **

**Step 3. Select Corporate Action Type **

 Use the Type filter to select the corporate action(s) you want to
process---Bonus, Consolidation, Dividend, or Split. You can select one
or multiple options from the dropdown.** **

**Step 4. Filter by Account and Holding Name **

 Select the required Account and Holding Name.** **

- The Account filter dropdown will display accounts linked to the
  selected entity or group; you can select one or multiple
  accounts.** **

<!-- -->

- The Holding Name filter dropdown allows you to select all holdings or
  specific ones based on the selected account.** **

**Step 5. Date Range Filters  **

You can either select a predefined date range from the dropdown or
manually set the "Date From" and "Date To" fields.** **

The Date Range filter offers several predefined options to simplify your
selection:** **

- **MTD (Month to Date): From the start of the current month to today\'s
  date. **

<!-- -->

- **Previous Month: From the 1st to the last day of the previous
  month. **

<!-- -->

- **QTD (Quarter to Date): From the start of the current quarter to
  today\'s date. **

<!-- -->

- **Previous Quarter: Covers the entire previous quarter, from the first
  to the last day. **

<!-- -->

- **Custom: Manually select a specific date range. **

**Example 1 for Date Range Filter: **

Selecting "Previous Month" in September will automatically set:** **

- Date From: August 1st** **

<!-- -->

- Date To: August 31st** **

However, if predefined options don't meet your needs, choose Custom and
manually set the "Date From" and "Date To" fields.** **

**Example 2 for Date Range Filter: **

To process corporate actions from August:** **

- Select "Previous Month" from the "Date Range" dropdown.** **

<!-- -->

- The system will auto-fill "Date From" as 01-Aug-2024 and "Date To" as
  31-Aug-2024.** **

For a custom range (e.g., August 15th to September 15th):** **

- Select "Custom" from the Date Range dropdown.** **

<!-- -->

- Manually set "Date From" to 15-Aug-2024 and "Date To" to
  15-Sep-2024.** **

** **

***Note : **When processing corporate actions for All Entities or a
Group, the custom date range is limited to 90 days. This restriction
does not apply to individual entities.** ***

**Step 6. Filter by Status **

- The Status filter defaults to \"All\", but you can use the filter to
  select "Processed" or "Unprocessed" corporate actions.** **

<!-- -->

- The system will display data based on the Status filter you
  choose.** **

** **

**Step 7. Setting the TDS Percentage **

- You can select a TDS value of 0% or 10% from the dropdown.** **

<!-- -->

- The system will automatically calculate the TDS for Dividend
  Payout transactions based on the selected percentage.** **

<!-- -->

- For other corporate actions like Split, Bonus, or Consolidation, the
  TDS column will stay blank.** **

***Note:** If needed, you can overwrite the TDS value for unprocessed
dividend payouts---just remember to click "Process" to save your
changes. *

**Step 8. Configure Payee/Payor/Bank Accounts **

- If you select Yes in the Process Payouts to Receivable A/Cs dropdown,
  the system will post dividend transactions in the Dividend Receivable
  -- Direct Equity ledger.** **

<!-- -->

- If you select No, the system will post dividend transactions in the
  default Payee/Payor/Bank ledger linked to the custodian account.** **

**Step 9. Processing Corporate Actions: **

- After setting all the necessary filters and configurations, proceed by
  initiating the corporate actions.** **

<!-- -->

- Once processed, the status of each corporate action will update to
  "Processed".** **

**Step 10. Review Processed Transactions: **

- Once processed, you can view the corporate actions in the Direct
  Equity module.** **

<!-- -->

- The processed actions will be recorded with the \'Ex date\' displayed
  in the Transaction Date column.
