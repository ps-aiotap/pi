To give you an accurate picture of your investment's performance,
here\'s how the current formula calculates the Time-Weighted Return
(TWR):

**Current Formula:** \
\
TWR is calculated using Holding Period Returns (HPR) as follows:

**• HPR Formula: **(EMV + CF) / Previous EMV** **(where EMV stands for
Ending Market Value and CF represents Cash Flow) \
 \
**• TWR Formula: **(HPR₁ \* HPR₂ \* HPR₃) − 1 

This approach works well in most situations, but there are certain edge
cases where it might not give you the most accurate results.
Specifically, it doesn't account for situations like disproportionate
purchases or sales, which can distort the outcome. For example, in one
instance, the current formula produced a TWR result of -36.1363%, even
though the actual loss was less than 1%. This can lead to confusion or
misrepresentation of performance in such edge cases.
