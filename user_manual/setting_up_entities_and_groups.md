**Let's get started!**

The first step to setting up your AV profile begins by creating entities
and groups on the AV system.

**What is an entity?**

An entity is defined as an individual tax identity - like a family
member, a trust, a partnership, and so on. 

**What is a group?** 

A group is a collection of two or more entities. For example, all
entities in a single family can be grouped to form a Family Group.

As per your needs, you can create a single entity or multiple entities
(aka groups) on our system. Create them manually, or add them all in,
together, via AV's ready-to-use Excel template for 'bulk uploads'.

**How to manually create a new entity or group **

**Step 1 **

Add entities or groups manually by heading to the \'Menu\', clicking on
\'Masters\', and then clicking \'Entity/Group\'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 2 **

Click the '+' sign at the top right corner of the page that opens. The
resulting pop-up box will let you create either an entity or a group as
per your requirement.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}

**Step 3**

Add relevant details for your entity or group.

*Note: Highlighted blocks indicate mandatory fields that cannot be left
blank.*

- **Entity Type:** Select your entity from a range of options like
  'Individual,' 'HUF,' 'Trust,' and so on. 

- **Default Share:** Choose how you wish to define the profit sharing of
  the Entity or Group, by \'Ownership\' shares or \'Profit Share\'. If
  not chosen, the system automatically selects this option as
  \'Ownership\'. 

- **Entity Name:** The requirement changes as per the entity type
  selected. For example, choosing 'Individual' requires you to add the
  first, middle, and last name of the person, while 'Trust' only
  requires name.  

- **Display Name:** Enter the full name of the entity. 

- **Enter Tax ID: **Enter the PAN card number (for Indian clients) and
  TIN (for US clients). 

- **Address and Email Address:** Enter relevant details as required. 

- **Accounting Year:** This depends on the region the entity is from.
  Choose the accounting year accordingly. 

- **Book Start Date:** This date defines the starting of the accounting
  entries. Accounting entries before the book start date for investment
  accounts will be one sided and for other ledgers, the closing balance
  till the book start date will be considered as the opening balance.

- **Default Accounting Template: **The AV system provides two default
  templates -- IFRS and US GAAP. You can also configure custom templates
  based on the default templates by going into 'Masters' from the
  'Menu,' and then selecting 'Chart of Accounts'. *For detailed
  information about this process, please refer to [this
  article](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018745738-Setting-up-and-customizing-chart-of-accounts-COA-using-the-base-GAAP-and-IFRS-templates).* 

- **Domicile and Currency: **After adding the domicile, you can select
  currencies for the \'Base Currency\' and \'Reporting Currency\'
  options, as needed. 

- **Benchmark: **You can set a Benchmark for Entity or Group level. It
  will be shown at Total level. Even at Primary & Secondary level in
  case report is run with Entity selected.

*Note :*

- *You can see this  benchmark on Capital Flow by Investment (on
  Analytics & Report Book) & Multiple Period widget (on Report Book). *

- *Make sure to double-check your book start date. It cannot be changed
  later. ***\**

**How to create entities or groups via a bulk upload **

**Step 6**

Export all your information with one click using a shortcut - the Excel
export button. Head to \'Uploads\' at the top right of the
\'Entity/Group\' page and click the \'Sample AV Upload File\' to
download our template onto your device.

*Tip: Both download and upload formats are the same, ensuring you have
an easy go of filling in your data. *

*\
\*

![](media/image8.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

*\*

**Step 7**

Add your details and save the changes. 

*\*

![](media/image9.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.248611111111111in"}

**Step 8**

Upload your file on the AV system; click the 'arrow' on the top right
corner of the screen.

*\*

![](media/image10.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="1.5555555555555556in"}

*\*

**Step 9**

All your details will be listed on the screen. Rows in green indicate no
errors and rows in pink indicate errors that can be fixed by clicking
the 'edit' icon.

*\*

![](media/image11.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="1.9381944444444446in"}

*\*

**Step 10**

Once all rows are green, click the checkbox next to each entity then
click the 'Process' button on the top right side of the page.

*Note: Alternatively, you can select the 'All' checkbox on the top left
to select all entries and process them together. *
