**Finding Accounts Master**

Create Payor Account from Accounts Master.\
Go to** Menu \> Masters \> Accounts**.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4784722222222224in"}

**Creating Custodian Account**

Create a custodian account and tag the default P/P/B as Payor account
created in Step 1.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.529166666666667in"}

**Post Mutual Fund Transaction**

You can now add the mutual fund transactions by clicking "+" sign
available in **Menu **\> **Transactions **\>\
**Mutual Fund screen**. Save the entry by clicking on the "Save" icon.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.7583333333333333in"}

**We hope you are now able to post Mutual Fund transactions manually.
