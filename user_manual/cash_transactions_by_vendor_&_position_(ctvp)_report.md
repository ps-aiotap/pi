**What is the Cash Transactions by Vendor & Position (CTVP) report?**

This report will enable you to collate transactions for the selected
Payee/Payor/Vendor/Position across all the selected bank accounts. It
will club the voucher data for the combination selected and will be a
great way to track voucher-wise activity for Payees, Payors, Vendors and
Investment Positions. 

**Generating the CTVP on the Report book**

**Step 1 **

Go to 'Menu', 'Report Book', then to 'Add Widget', and finally, to the
'General Ledger' widget.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 2 **

Click the filter icon to get a pop-up that lets you select your report
type. There, choose 'Cash Transactions by Vendor & Position'. 

Select \'Accounts\', \'Payee/Payor/Vendor/Positions\' to generate this
report for Payees/ Payors/ Vendors/ Positions or a combination thereof.
Based on the selected category, select the  \'Payee/Payor/Vendor
(Accounts)/Positions\' accounts and other details from the filter, then
click 'Process' to generate the report.

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.2958333333333334in"}

Notes:

 • In other fields, the \'Show Multiple Accounts\' option helps you
expand the voucher details to include other legs of the transactions
other than a bank, which is the secondary grouping of this report.

• Level 3 shows you the voucher details and two additional columns,
namely 'dr' and 'cr', which will appear on the report only when the
'Show Multiple Accounts\' is checked.

 • '-- Multiple --' on the report means that more than two ledger
accounts are impacted by a particular transaction, voucher details of
which can be viewed in the expanded form, i.e., level 3.

• \'Hide Account Detail\' will hide the subscript of the account details
for multiple columns. 

**Generating the CTVP report on the General Ledger (Pro)**

**Step 3 **

Head to 'Menu', then 'General Ledger (Pro)', and finally to 'Cash
Transactions by Vendor & Position\' report.

![](media/image4.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 4 **

Click the filter icon and select the appropriate filters to get the
desired report and click on the \'Process\' to fetch your report.

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.2958333333333334in"}

**Note:**  The filters for the CTVP report on Report Book as well as
General Ledger (Pro) and the primary working remains the same for both.
