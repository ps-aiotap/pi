**About the Chart of Accounts**

The Chart of Accounts (COA) gives you the flexibility to create your own
custom template using either GAAP or IFRS templates as the base. You can
apply these custom templates to multiple entities or groups.

**Configure your template for the COA** 

The AV system offers two accounting templates by default - GAAP and
IFRS - which cannot be altered in any way. Use either template as a base
to create your custom template. 

**Step 1 **

Go to 'Masters', then 'Chart of Accounts'. 

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 2 **

Click the "+" sign on at the top right corner. In the pop-up 'Add/Edit'
dialogue box, select your base template, enter your custom template
name, select either 'Entity' or 'Group', and then select the Entity or
Group for which you want to assign the custom COA. Finally, click save.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.189583333333333in"}

**Step 3 **

To see the list of templates for any entity, select their name from the
dropdown, then hit the 'Check' button.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="1.6784722222222221in"}

Note:  You will be able to see the list of default as well as custom
templates. '+' icon.

**Edit, copy, or export your template** 

**Step 4 **

After creating new templates, you have the flexibility to edit, copy or
export them.

**Step 5 **

You can also configure your custom created templates, by heading to the
'Configure Template' tab.

![](media/image4.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"} 

**Step 6 **

Select the 'Entity' and the 'Template', then click the check icon.

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.467361111111111in"}

**Step 7 **

To customise, simply click and drag the items under each section and
place them under a new group head. Alternatively, you can also select
multiple items at the same time.

**Step 8 **

You can also 'right-click' to add another grouping or edit each item's
name.

**Step 9 **

Once you're done with the configuration, click 'Save'.

![](media/image6.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

Note:  Once you have applied custom templates to an Entity/Group, you
can choose a custom template to generate your 'Balance Sheet' under
'General Ledger'. Refer to [\"General Ledger :Balance
Sheet" ](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4414912561041-General-Legder-Balance-Sheet)article
to understand it in detail.

**We hope you are now ready to successfully set up and configure the
COA template. Still have questions? Feel free to reach out to AV\'s
Customer Success Team.**
