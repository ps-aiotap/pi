For NRI clients, TDS must be deducted on all Sell or Redemption
transactions. The system now provides a TDS Amount field in the Mutual
Fund, Direct Equity, and Fixed Income modules for these transaction
types.

**Step 1:** File Path: **Menu **\> **Transactions**

**Step 2: **Navigate to the relevant transaction screen for TDS field:

Go to the relevant module and choose the correct transaction type:

- In the Mutual Fund module, select Redemption or Switch Out

- In the Direct Equity module, select Sell

- In the Fixed Income module, select Sell for OFCD & Bonds

- In the Fixed Income module, select Sell for Certificate of Deposit &
  Commercial Paper

**Step 3: **Locate the TDS Amount Field\
You'll find the TDS Amount field under the "Taxes & Charges ledger,"
just below "Transaction charge" when you add or edit a transaction for:

- Redemption

- Switch Out (Mutual Fund)

- Sell (Direct Equity)

- Sell (Fixed Income - OFCD & Bonds)

- For Fixed Income - Certificate of Deposit & Commercial Paper, the TDS
  field is added below "Other Charge".

                    ![A screenshot of a computer AI-generated content

height="3.0680555555555555in"}

**Step 4: **Enter TDS Amount

- Enter the TDS Amount applicable for the transaction.

- Note: The system does not allow negative values for TDS.

- In the voucher, the TDS amount will be posted to the Debit side of the
  ledger account under \"Taxes Receivable -- TDS.\"\

***Key Points to Remember***

- *TDS Amount cannot be negative for any transaction type.*

- *Always enter the TDS Amount in the correct field to prevent errors.*

- *The TDS Amount will reflect in exports, vouchers, ledger accounts,
  and reports.*

- *In the Private Equity module, TDS will be treated as an asset and
  will be adjusted based on Tax Withheld on Distribution.*

**Conclusion**

With the TDS Amount field now available in the Mutual Fund, Direct
Equity, Fixed Income, and Private Equity modules, you can easily meet
NRI tax requirements and manage TDS deductions without hassle. Follow
the steps above to record and report TDS accurately for all supported
transaction types.
