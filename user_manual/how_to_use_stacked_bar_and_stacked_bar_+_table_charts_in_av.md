The Bar & Table widget in the AV system now offers two powerful
visualization options---**Stacked Bar** and **Stacked Bar + Table**.
These chart types are designed to help you visually compare data across
multiple categories and subcategories with clarity and precision.

**Step 1: Access the Bar & Table Widget**

- Go to **Menu \> Report Book \> Bar & Table widget**.

*(Note - If your dashboard is locked, unlock it first to add a new Bar &
Table widget.)*

- Once added, open the **Chart Type **dropdown. You'll now see two new
  chart options available:

  - Stacked Bar

  - Stacked Bar + Table

**Step 2: Configure Your View with Filters**

Use the available filters to shape your chart exactly the way you need:

- Click on the **Filters **icon (![](media/image2.png){width="0.1875in"
  height="0.1527777777777778in"})

<!-- -->

- **Primary Grouping**: Defines the main categories along the X-axis
  (e.g., *Asset Class*, *Account*, *Advisor*).

- **Secondary Grouping**: Determines how each bar is divided into
  segments, based on a secondary category within the selected Primary
  Grouping (e.g., *Advisor* or *Asset Class*).

- **Primary & Secondary Sub-Groups: **Lets you add further granularity
  within each grouping (e.g., *Bank Cash*, *Bonds and Debentures*).

- **Chart Value**: Determines the values plotted on the bars (e.g.,
  Market Value, Cost, Income).

- **Additional Chart Label**: Adds a secondary label (e.g., % of Total
  or Currency).

**Step 3: Navigating the Stacked Bar Chart**

A **Stacked Bar Chart** is a type of bar graph where each bar shows a
total value, and the bar is divided into segments that represent the
sub-categories making up that total.

- The **Primary Grouping** sets the categories displayed along the
  X-axis.

<!-- -->

- Each bar is then **segmented** based on the selected **Secondary
  Grouping**, showing how each part contributes to the whole.

- Hover over any bar to reveal a **tooltip**---a small pop-up box that
  displays detailed information for that segment based on your selected
  filters. Data labels, such as "exact values" or "percentages", will
  appear according to your preferences.

**Step 4: Customize the Chart/Bar Using the 3-Dot Menu**

Use the **3-dot menu** on the widget to personalize how your chart
appears. The following customization options are available:

- **Show Currency Symbol**: Display currency symbols alongside numeric
  values.

- **Show Chart Label**: Enables or disables the display of category
  labels on the chart.

- **Show Data Labels**: Show the actual data values within each bar
  segment.

- **Show Legend**: Add a legend to explain chart segments, with
  placement options (Bottom, Left, or Right).

- **Show Footer Notes**: Display any custom notes you\'ve added to the
  chart.

**Step 5: Understanding the Stacked Bar + Table Chart**

The **Stacked Bar + Table** chart combines two views in a single widget:
a visual **Stacked Bar Chart** and a detailed **data Table**.

You can interact with this chart in two ways:

**Chart View**: Customize the visual layout and appearance of the
stacked bars.

**Table View**: Explore drilled-down data in a structured, tabular
format.

**Additional Tips: Table-Specific Features**

- You can **expand or collapse hierarchical rows** to drill down into
  details within the primary grouping.

- You can filter or sort the data based on "Level 1" or "Level 2"
  groupings or create a custom filter to categorize the information as
  needed.
