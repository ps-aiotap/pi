**Payee, payor, and vendor accounts on the AV system**

While payee/payor accounts are specific to an entity; vendors are common
across all entities within the system. This article will help you set up
a payee, payor, or vendor account, and link them to a bank transaction.

**Setting up payee/payor accounts**

**Step 1 **

Go to \'Masters\' in the \'Menu\', and then to \'Accounts\'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 2 **

Click the "+" in the top right corner to access a pop-up page.

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 3 **

In the \'Class : Account Type\' dropdown, choose between \'Asset :
Payor\' or \'Liability : Payee\'.  

  ![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.997916666666667in"}

- 'Asset : Payor' creates a payor for withdrawals or deposits, and can
  also be used like an 'Accounts Receivable' under 'Assets' on your
  'Balance Sheet' when used in place of a bank on certain transactions.

- "Liability: Payee" can also be used like 'Accounts Payable' under
  'Liabilities' on your 'Balance Sheet' when used in place of a bank on
  certain transactions.

*Tip: Find the 'Balance Sheet' under 'General Ledger' in your 'Menu'.
Learn more about the balance sheet from the 'General Ledger - Balance
Sheet' article. *

**Step 4 **

Fill in your information and click 'Save'.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

*Note: Don't forget to fill in all fields marked with orange in this and
subsequent steps; this indicates a mandatory cell. *

**Setting up the vendor account**

**Step 5**

Head to 'Masters' in the 'Menu', then to 'Vendors'.

![](media/image5.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 6 **

Click the \'+\' icon, fill in your information, and hit \'Save\' to
create the vendor account.

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Assigning payees/payors/vendors**

Payees/Payors/Vendors can be assigned or updated for bank/cash
transactions, by following the below steps:

**Step 7 **

Go to \'Transactions\' from the \'Menu\', then click \'Bank, Credit Card
& Cash\'.

![](media/image7.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 8 **

Click '+' in the top right-hand corner. 

*Tip: Select the 'Edit' symbol on an existing transaction to alter the
payee/payor/vendor field. *

![](media/image8.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**Step 9 **

Fill in your information, then click the 'Save' icon.

![](media/image9.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

vendors. Still have questions? Feel free to reach out to AV\'s Customer
Success Team.**
