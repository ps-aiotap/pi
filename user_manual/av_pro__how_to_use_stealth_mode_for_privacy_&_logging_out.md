**Stealth Mode for Privacy**

Stealth Mode helps protect your privacy by masking absolute numbers when
the app is shared or viewed by others. Once activated, it conceals
absolute values and displays only high-level summaries.

- To activate Stealth Mode, tap the lock icon in the top-right corner of
  the screen.

- To deactivate Stealth Mode, tap the lock icon again to restore full
  visibility of your financial data.

**Logging Out**

To log out of the app, navigate to the **Burger Menu (☰)** and
click **Log Out**.

This will sign you out of the app, ensuring that your session has
securely ended.
