A majority of AV analytics and General Ledger reports can be reviewed
with its robust filtering and grouping.

**Report filter combinations available**

All reports can be generated with the following filter-flow.

- For any Entity or Group of Entities.

- With Partnership look-through.

- Consolidated by a Primary & Secondary grouping that consists of a
  down-selection across Asset Class, Strategy, Sector, Advisor,
  Liquidity, Geography, Currency, Holding and Position.

- For a time period:

  - As on date

  - Single date range (custom period)

  - Multi-select date range

- In any Currency (default is entity or group reporting currency).

- By a Denomination (millions, thousands etc.).
