A wash sale occurs when you sell a security at a loss and then purchase
that same security or "substantially identical" securities within 30
days (before or after the sale date).

**Scenarios**

There are 2 scenarios for the wash sales activity. Below are the
details:

- **[Scenario 1:]{.underline}** In this scenario, the custodian
  statement **does not** reflect the **"Realized Loss" **after the wash
  sales effect. The loss is added up to the cost basis of the securities
  repurchased.

- **[Scenario 2:]{.underline}** In this scenario, the custodian
  statement **does** reflect the **"Realized Loss" **after the wash
  sales effect.
