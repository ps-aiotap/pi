The Investment Policy Statement, or IPS, lets you define and configure
multiple investment policies on the AV system, that too, with any
grouping combinations of your choice, including Asset Class, Strategy,
and more. Learn how to access this feature, and add it as a widget on
your dashboard and Report Book.

**Understanding IPS**

An Investment Policy Statement (IPS) or Investment Mandate provides
guidance to portfolio managers or allocators to make portfolio
decisions. It also helps investors track their investments in a
particular asset class, strategy, sector, and more, helping them
minimize risk by keeping a regular check on the deviation from the
stated investment policy. Specific information on asset allocation, risk
tolerance, and more are also included in the IPS, to compare the ideal
allocation and returns with actuals.

**Configuring IPS profile**

 **Step 1**

 Click the '+' icon to add the 'Investment Policy' master.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="0.9930555555555556in"}

**Step 2**

A new screen will appear, to define the IPS master.

*Note: You can only define IPS for these groupings - Advisor, Asset
Class, Sector, Strategy, Currency, Geography, Liquidity, and User
defined tags.*

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.5416666666666665in"}

- **Profile Name**: This must be a unique name.

- **Description**: State how you'd like to describe your profile in this
  optional field.

- **Expected Allocation (%)**: It is the expected allocation defined by
  you as per your investment objective and goals.

- **Allocation Tolerance +/- (%)**: This is the tolerance defined over
  expected allocation, to define the range of allocation. For example,
  if you've put expected allocation for Equity as 35% and allocation
  tolerance as 5%, your minimum expected allocation will be 30% (35% -
  5%) and maximum expected allocation will be 40% (35% + 5%).

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.4659722222222222in"}

- **Benchmark Return Type**: Choose between \'Absolute\' (percentage
  value in an absolute number) or \'Relative\' (percentage value
  relative to Benchmark \[Index\]).

- **Benchmark/Base Return**: Define expected/comparison return for
  investment;\
  depending upon the 'Benchmark Return Type' selected, you can either
  enter the absolute percentage or select the benchmark (index) as an
  expected return.

- **Return Tolerance (%)**: It is the expected return above the
  'Benchmark/Base' return.

  - Select 'Relative' in 'Benchmark Return Type' and your
    'Benchmark/Base Return' will show the list of indices to select, and
    the 'Return Tolerance' is the  spread on top of the Benchmark/Base
    Return.

- 

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in" height="2.50625in"}

*Caption for the screenshot: Selecting a relative benchmark, i.e., S&P
500, with a return tolerance of 0.50 means you are expecting a return
which equals a return percentage of S&P 500 + 0.50%.*

- - Select 'Absolute' in 'Benchmark Return Type' and while you can enter
    the absolute percentage in 'Benchmark/Base Return', you will not be
    allowed to enter the 'Return Tolerance'.

- 

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.5104166666666665in"}

*Caption for the screenshot: Selecting an absolute return of 2% means
you are expecting a return of 2%.*

*Note: The 'Total', aka the total expected allocation, of a profile must
be 100%.*

*Tip: You can define multiple investment policies at different grouping
such as Asset Class, Sector, Strategy, etc., under the same profile.*

**Where to view IPS details**

**Step 1**

See your IPS reports in the Report Book. Go to \'Report Book\' from the
\'Menu\' and find the \'Investment Policy\' from your widget section. 

*To learn how to set up a dashboard and widgets on the Report Book,
please refer to the [Report book explained
article](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403260437393-The-AV-Report-Book-explained).*

![](media/image8.png){width="2.3333333333333335in"
height="3.923611111111111in"}

**Step 2**

 Click the filter icon, select the right filters to get data on your
screen.

*Note: In the widget filter section of your IPS widget, columns like
'Expected Allocation %', 'Actual Allocation %', and 'Market Value' have
been selected by default. You need to manually select other items to
process and view your data.*

![](media/image9.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.348611111111111in"}

**Step 3**

Use the \'Investment Universe Filter\' and \'Sub Filter\' dropdowns to
further filter information which appears according to the primary
grouping selected. For example, if you've selected 'Asset-Class' as your
primary grouping and you want to eliminate the 'Personal Cash' strategy,
then select 'Strategy' in your Investment Universe filter and select all
options except 'Personal Cash' in the sub filter.

![](media/image10.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.3805555555555555in"}

**Step 4**

Click 'Process' and a sample IPS will appear for analysis.

![](media/image11.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.0395833333333333in"}

*Note: Underlined percentages indicate data beyond tolerance limits. *

- *For actual allocation, this happens when the actual allocation
  percentage is either less than the minimum allocation percentage or
  more than the maximum allocation.*

- *The percentage of returns is highlighted when the actual return
  percentage is less than the expected return.*

*Tip: You can add multiple IPS widgets on a dashboard to get a
comprehensive view of your investment data.*
