*Is your system running slowly? Is data not getting refreshed properly?
Try clearing your browser cache!*

**What are cached files?**

Cached files/data are the information stored in a temporary storage
location on your computer or device after you open an app or visit a
website.

**Why does your browser store cached files?**

Caching improves and speeds up browsing. Most browsers use the cache to
store data for all websites visited by you. Its function is to make the
loading of your next visit to the site faster than the first time.

**Why you must clear your browser cache?**

- **It takes up storage:** Because caches use your device\'s local
  storage, which might occupy space needed for other purposes. If the
  storage becomes too full, that can decrease your computer\'s speed. It
  can also reduce the speed of your applications and web browsers.

- **It stops loading updated content: **Though most websites and
  applications can force caches to download the updated files and
  content, those systems don\'t always work. It\'s possible that the
  data your cache saves might stop websites from showing you their
  latest content.

**Why is it important to clear cached files?**

If you encounter wrong results or sluggish performance, consider
clearing your browser cache. This is particularly important after AV
code updates are released, because old files may interfere with the code
changes causing issues like inaccurate figures or odd browser behavior.
Clearing your cache will ensure optimum system performance and correct
results.

**How to clear the browser cache on Google Chrome?**

**Step 1 **

In your browser, in the top right corner select the three dots next to
the Google sign-in.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.3465277777777778in"}

**Step 2**

In the dropdown, scroll down to 'More tools' and select 'Clear browsing
data'.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.9743055555555555in"}

Note: You can even use the shortcut for this, by clicking Ctrl +Shift+
Delete. 

**Step 3**

Make sure that only the 'Cached images and files' option is selected,
then click 'Clear data'.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.3805555555555555in"}

Note: This will not sign you out of any active sessions; this is only a
possible solution for slow system performance or incorrect results. 

**How to clear the browser cache on Firefox?**

**Step 4**

Click the menu icon and select 'Settings'.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.3819444444444446in"}

**Step 5**

Select the 'Privacy & Security' panel.

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.8027777777777776in"}

**Step 6**

In the 'Cookies and Site Data' section, click 'Clear Data', then select
'Cached Web Content' before clicking the 'Clear' button.

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.279166666666667in"}

Tips:

- We recommend clearing your browser cache on a weekly basis.

- For a seamless AV experience, use it on the Google or Firefox browser.
