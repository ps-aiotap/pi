You can now track your income and expenses over time using
the **Sequential Income Statement**. Available under the General Ledger
widget in the **Report Book**, this report provides a detailed breakdown
of your financial performance across multiple time periods.

**Step 1: How to Access the GL widget in the Report Book?**

- Navigate to **Menu \> Report Book**.

- Find the **General Ledger** widget.* *

- **Unlock the dashboard**, then click on **Add Widget** and drag
  the **General Ledger widget **to the **main screen**.\
  ![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
  height="3.0756944444444443in"}

**Step 2**: **Select the Report Type:**

-  Click on the **Filters **icon (![](media/image2.png){width="0.1875in"
  height="0.1527777777777778in"})

- In the **Report Type** dropdown, select **\"Income Statement\".**

- You\'ll find this option listed just after **\"Change in Net
  Worth\"**.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0805555555555557in"}

**Step 3: Set the Report Format:**

- In the **Report **dropdown, select **\"Sequential\".**

- This format displays your **income and expenses **across **multiple
  periods**, allowing you to **track **changes over time.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0805555555555557in"}

**Step 4: Select an Entity or Group:**

- Use the **Entity/Group** dropdown to select the relevant individual
  entity or group.

- This dropdown includes all available entities and groups you've
  created, along with an option to select **"All Entities"** if you want
  a consolidated view.

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0805555555555557in"}

 

**Step 5: Selecting Accounts:**

- Click on **Accounts** to open a dropdown showing all
  available **income and expense accounts**---including both
  system-generated and custom-created ones.

- You can **filter and select** the accounts you want to view for
  a **comparative and sequential analysis**.

- You can also **refine your selection of accounts** using **three
  levels of grouping**:

1.  **Income or Expense**

2.  **Primary Grouping**

3.  **Secondary Grouping**

 

- For example, under **Income**, you might select **Earned Income**,
  which further breaks down into sub-categories like **Salary
  Invoice**, **Service Income**, **Gratuity**, **HRA**, **Consulting
  Income**, etc. Pick the respective income and expense accounts and
  select the ones that are most relevant to your reporting needs.

 ![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0756944444444443in"}

 

**Step 6: Set the Reporting Period**

**1) As On Date:**

- The **'As on date'** can be set to any specific date for which you
  want to view the report.

- By default, it is set to the current date.

- You can update it using the **date picker**.* *

* *

![](media/image7.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0805555555555557in"}

** **

**2) Period:**

- Under the **Period** filter, choose
  between **Monthly** or **Quarterly** reporting.

 

**3) Number of Periods:**

- You can choose a **fixed number of periods**---anywhere from **1 to
  12** (e.g., 2 periods, 8 periods, FYTD, CYTD).

- You can also select **FYTD** (Financial Year-To-Date)
  or **CYTD** (Calendar Year-To-Date) to view data from the beginning of
  the financial or calendar year up to the selected \"As on Date\".

![](media/image8.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0805555555555557in"}**                                       
                    **

**Monthly Example:**

- If you select Monthly and choose 3 periods, the report will show
  income and expense columns for the last 3 months (e.g., Jan, Feb,
  Mar).**               **

**FYTD Example: April to as on date**

- If you select Monthly or any other period and choose FYTD, the report
  will display columns from the start of the financial year (e.g.,
  April) up to the As on Date you've set.

![](media/image9.png){width="6.268055555555556in" height="2.725in"}

** **

**CYTD Example: January to as on date**

- If you select Monthly or any other period and choose CYTD, the report
  will display columns from the start of the financial year (e.g.,
  April) up to the As on Date you've set.

![](media/image10.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.076388888888889in"}

**Step 7: Currency & Denomination:**

- Select your preferred **currency** from the dropdown (e.g., INR, USD).

- Choose the **denomination** in which you want to view the report
  (e.g., Thousands, Lakhs, etc.).

**Step 8: Other Fields:**

- The **Other Fields** dropdown offers two options:

- **Hide Acct. details **- Displays the report at the **position level
  only**, without showing a drilled-down view of individual income and
  expense accounts.

- **Summary **- Displays the report in **full detail**, showing **each
  income and expense** entry along with its **drill-down.**

** **

![](media/image8.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.0805555555555557in"}

 

***You've now got everything you need to generate and customize a
Sequential Income Statement in Asset Vantage. Use the filters to tailor
the view to your needs and get clear insights into your income and
expenses over time.***
