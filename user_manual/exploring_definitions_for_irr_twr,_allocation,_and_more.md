**What\'s in the article?**

This article explains everything you need to know about various
important definitions as on the AV system, including terms like \'IRR\',
\'allocation\', and more. 

 

**Wealth Register**

This is an as-on-date report of holdings which includes Units, Cost
Basis, Market Value and Since Inception Performance.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="0.5541666666666667in"}

**IRR %**

The Internal Rate of Return (IRR) is computed based on cashflows, dates
of cashflows, and market values. It provides a measure for evaluating
the performance of investments. Reports which have the IRR%: Wealth
Register, Private Equity, Portfolio Performance.

**PME Benchmark IRR%**

This indicates the performance of the selected benchmark as per
cashflows, dates, and market value of the position. Reports which have
PME Benchmark IRR%: Private Equity, Portfolio Performance, Multi-Period
Performance.

**Allocation%**

This is the market value of the position divided by the market value of
the total wealth, in percentage terms. Report which has Allocation %:
Wealth Register.

**Quantity**

This is the number of units held for any particular investment as on the
date. Reports which have Quantity Wealth Register, Gains Report.

**Average Cost**

This is the average purchase cost per unit (which is equal to the
purchase value/quantity held). Reports which have the Average Cost:
Wealth Register, Gains Report.

**Purchase Value**

This refers to the cost basis of the units held as on the date selected.
Reports which have the Purchase Value: Wealth Register, Gains Report.

**Value as on selected date**

This indicates the market value of the position held as on the selected
date or last available market price. Reports which have the Value as on
selected date: Wealth Register, Gains Report.  

Note:  For 'Bonds', the market price is the 'Clean price', i.e., without
accrued interest, and accrued interest is shown in a separate column. 

**Un-realized Gain/Loss (Currency)**

This is the difference between the market value and cost basis of the
unsold units/investments as on date selected. This data is presented in
numerical values. Reports which have the Un-realized Gain/Loss: Wealth
Register, Gains Report.

**Un-realized Gain/Loss %**

This is the un-realized Gain/Loss, in percentage terms. Reports which
have the Un-realized Gain/Loss: Wealth Register, Gains Report.

**Portfolio Performance**

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in" height="0.625in"}

This gives a range-bound performance analysis of the portfolio based on
the period selected.

Following are the details of each column:

- **Cost Basis on Start Date**: This is the cost value of the position
  as of the start date.

- **Cost Basis on End Date: **This is the cost value of the position as
  of the end date. 

- **Value on Start Date: **This refers to the market value of
  units/investments held as of the start date.

- **Value on End Date: **This is the market value of units/investments
  held as of the end date.

Notes:

1\. Bond valuations are always on the clean price for both 'Value on
Start Date' and Value on End Date'.

2\. Opening values are derived by using closing prices of the previous
day for both cost and market value.

**Return %**

This is the absolute return within the period - in percentage terms -
for the investment.

**Realized Gain/Loss in (Currency)**

This is the total gain on sold units in between the period. The sales
proceeds will be compared with the market value on the start date.
Reports which have the Realised Gain/Loss: Performance Report, Gains
Report.

**Dividend/Interest/Rental**

These are all the income pay-outs (dividend, interest, rental income,
and more) received during the period for the position. Reports which
have the Dividend/Interest/Rental: Performance Report, Gains Report.

**Accrued Income**

This is the accrued income from the last Interest pay-out date or from
the report date, whichever is latest. Reports which have the Accrued
Income: Performance Report, Gains Report.

**Total Gain**

This is the sum of realized gains, unrealized gains, and all pay-outs
(dividend, interest, rental income, etc.) in the period. Reports which
have the Total Gain: Performance Report, Gains Report.

**TWR %**

The Time Weighted Return (TWR) provides a total return (geometrically
linked) of the investment within the selected period.

**Benchmark TWR %**

The Time Weighted Return provides a total return (geometrically linked)
of the benchmark index within the selected period.

This report will give the details of capital gains and losses for
different asset classes broken into each tax lot.

Following are the details of each column:

**Short Term Gain**

Short-term capital gain/loss occurs if purchase and sale is within a
short term period. (= Sell Price -- Buy Price).

**Long Term Gain**

Long-term capital gain/loss occurs if purchase and sale is within a long
term period. (= Sell Price -- Buy Price).

**Total Capital Gain/Loss**

This is the sum of short term and long term capital gains/losses.

**Indexation Gain/Loss**

This refers to the Gain/Loss as per indexation valuation. (This is
applicable only to India and other geographies).

**Total Gain/Loss**

This is the sum of realized gains/losses, unrealized gains gains/losses,
and all pay-outs (dividend, interest, rental income, and more).

**Holding Period (Years)**

This is the holding period of the lot in years (= (Sell Date for sold
tax-lots or End Date for Unsold tax-lots - Buy Date) / 365).

**Days to Long Term**

This indicates the days remining to reach the holding in long term.

**Long Term Date**

This is the future date on which a specific tax-lot will turn into a
long term holding.

**Open Quantity**

This is the available quantity.

**Lot ID**

This is the system generated Lot ID.

**Buy Price/Adjusted Price**

This is the buy price of the lot or adjusted buy price due to cost basis
adjustments, Return of Capital, amortization, or any corporate actions
like Split, Consolidation, and Merger/Demerger.

**Cost Basis / Tax Basis**

Buy Price/Adjusted Price \* Sell Quantity

Sell Price / Current Price \* Sell Quantity

**Purchase Index**

Value of Purchase index applicable only to India and other geographies.

**Sell Index**

Value of Sell index applicable only to India and other geographies.

**Purchase Index Value**

Purchase value as per Indexation (applicable for India and/or other
geographies).

**Private Equity Report**

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="0.2972222222222222in"}

The private equity report gives details of commitments, unfunded
commitments, drawdowns, and distribution, along with performance.

Following are the details of each column:

**Commitment**

This is the total commitment for private equity investments.

**Unfunded Commitment**

This indicates the remaining commitment to be invested. (= Commitment --
Total of Drawdown transactions). There's also an option available to add
recallable distributions in unfunded commitment.

**Amount Called**

This is the sum of all drawdown amounts.

**Called %**

This is the percentage of drawdowns made compared to the commitment
amount (= Amount Called / Commitment).

**Amount Distributed**

This is the sum of all distributed amounts.

**Distributed %**

This refers to the percentage of distribution made, compared to the
commitment amount (=Amount Distributed / Commitment).

**Balance Recallable Capital**

This is the sum of all positive and negative recallable distribution
transactions.

**Recallable Distributions**

This indicates the sum of all positive recallable distribution
transactions.

**Cost Basis**

This is the running ledger balance of PE investment (custodian) account.

**Invested Cost**

The invested cost is computed as = Amount Called - Balance Recallable
Capital + Expense.

**Capital Account Value**

This is the market value or cost basis of remaining investment (= Market
Value or Running ledger balance of investment account).

**CAV%**

This is the percentage of capital account value compared to the
commitment amount (= Capital Account Value / Commitment).

**(CAV + Dist.) as % of Called**

= (Capital Account Value + Amount Distributed) / Amount Called)

**Income**

This is the sum of all income transactions.

**Expense**

This is the sum of all expense transactions.

**DV/PI**

This refers to the Distributed Value to Paid-In ratio (=Amount
Distributed / Amount Called).

**TV/PI**

This is the Total Value to Paid-In ratio = \[(Capital Account Value +
Amount Distributed) / Amount Called)\].

**Entity / Group**

An "Entity" is defined as a unique tax identity, for example, a family
member, a trust, a partnership etc. A "Group" is a collection of two or
more entities, for example all entities in a single family can be
grouped to form a Family Group.

**Accounting Year**

The accounting year is your tax/financial year for the purpose of
creating books of accounts. Example: 1st January to 31st December or 1st
April to 31st March.

**Domicile**

The domicile of an entity is the place where you maintain your permanent
home. The domicile is important for legal purposes such as paying taxes,
voting, and claiming benefits.

**Base Currency**

For accounting purposes, you'd use the base currency as your domestic
currency or accounting currency to represent all profits and losses in
your account books.

**Reporting Currency**

Usually, base and reporting currency are the same. However, you could
choose a different reporting currency and set that as a default in the
AV system.

**Holding Pattern in percentage**

This is relevant for partnership entities with inter-entity cross and
sub holdings. You can define ownership percentages to
determine/represent holding patterns.

**Book Start Date**

In AV, the book start date is the start of the accounting year and
denotes the activation of the accounting engine. Hence, books of
accounts are created from this day forward.\
Any transaction in your investment accounts that are prior to the book
start date creates a one-sided accounting entry. All running ledger
balances (including but not limited to cash accounts) adding up to a day
prior to the book start date will be considered as opening balance as of
the book start date.

Example: Entity A has been set up with a book start date of January 1st,
2020. Entity A has an open brokerage / Custodian account with JPM
Morgan. The ending cash balance of JPM Morgan as of December 31st, 2019,
will be considered as the opening balance as of January 1st, 2020 (the
book start date).

Note:  Once chosen, the book start date cannot be changed to an earlier
or a later date. 

**Advisors**

You can tag an advisor in AV for reporting purposes, which is any person
or company involved in advising or investing capital for investors.

**Account**

This is the name of the brokerage/custodian account.

**Account Type**

The account type helps to create different masters related to
investments, banks, ledgers, payee, and payors, which will be
referenced, tagged and used throughout your system while uploading
transactions and viewing analytical reports. For the purpose of scoping
out implementation, accounts can broadly be of two types - (1) an
Investment account (like a brokerage, custodian, hedge fund, real
estate, or any asset account), and, (2) a bank account or cash account.

**No of years of history to implement**

Implementation in AV is broadly split into 2 parts:

\(1\) Investment-related history, which includes all historical
transactions / open tax lots in the portfolio related to the open
positions on the book start date\
(2) Accounting transactions, which include all bank/cash transactions
from the book start date.

Note:  The number of years of history could vary from account to account
in an entity. 

**Total no of historical transactions**

Implementation in AV is broadly split into 2 parts:\
(1) Investment-related history, which includes all historical
transactions / open tax lots in the portfolio related to the open
positions on the book start date.\
(2) Accounting transactions, which include all bank/cash transactions
from the book start date.

**Asset Class**

An asset class is a grouping of investments that exhibit similar
characteristics and are subject to the same laws and regulations. These
are defined as equities, mutual funds, fixed income, cash, alternative
investments, real estate, and derivatives in the AV system. While the AV
system has asset classes as pre-set tags, you can also create custom
tags for asset classes for reporting requirements.

**Detailed / Managed**

For accounting and reporting purposes, there are two ways to represent
investment accounts in AV:\
Detailed: See all the holdings and transactions within an account --
these could be a combination of the different asset class transactions
reflected in one portfolio.\
Managed: See the entire holding in an account at a summary level, i.e.,
transactions of the different asset classes will be reflected as a
single-line summary.

**Lot Relief Method**

A tax lot relief method determines which lot of stock or securities ---
and its associated cost basis --- is used in computing the gain or loss
on a sale and whether that gain or loss is long- or short-term.

**Payee/Payor**

The payor lets you create masters for all your broker and custodian
accounts from which your custodian accounts are credited; payee lets you
create masters for liability.

**Vendor Master**

The vendor master automatically reflects the vendor's name on checks,
reducing the manual effort involved in writing these.
