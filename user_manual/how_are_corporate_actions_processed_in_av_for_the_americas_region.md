**Stock Split**\
\
A stock split is a corporate action in which a company increases the
number of its outstanding shares by issuing additional shares to current
shareholders.

- Number of outstanding shares increases.

- Share price decreases proportionally.

- **Market Capitalization remains unchanged.**

**Example:**\
\
NVIDIA Corporation. has 600 shares trading at \$1,217.9 each on
7^th^ June 2024.

- Market Capitalization = 600 × \$1,217.9 = \$730,740.00.

If the company announces a **10-for-1 split**:

- Shares outstanding increased by 10 times to **6000**.

- Share price is reduced to **\$121.79**.

- Market Capitalization = 6000 × \$121.79 = **\$730,740.00**.

**Reverse Stock Split**

A reverse stock split reduces the number of outstanding shares by
consolidating them.

**Example:**\
 American Rebel Holdings Inc has 6000 shares trading at \$0.65 each.

- Market Capitalization = 6000 × \$0.65 = \$3,900.00.

After a **1-for-20 reverse split**:

- New shares outstanding = 6000 ÷ 20 = **300**.

- New share price = \$0.65 × 20 = **\$13**.

- Market Cap = 300 × \$13 = **\$3900**.

**Merger**

**Non-Taxable Merger**

In a non-taxable merger, cost basis carries forward.

**Example:**

- Investor holds 200 shares of Discover Financial Services.

  - Cost = \$22,480.00

  - Market Value = \$36,510.00

- Exchange ratio: 1 Discover Financial Services = 1.0192 Capital One.

- Cash In Lieu: \$20 per share 

**After Merger:**

- Receives 203 Capital shares + \$16.8 (0.84 \* \$20) cash in lieu. Cash
  in Lieu is given for the fractional shares that are not distributed. 

- Cost basis of \$22,387.36 transfers to new holdings with remaining
  Cost allocated to Cash in lieu.

**Taxable Merger**

In a taxable merger, shareholders recognize gains/losses, and it
triggers capital gains liability.

**Example:**

- Investors hold 1000 shares of Ferroglobe PLC.

  - Cost = \$9075

  - Market Value = \$10,000

- Receives:

  - 85 Weatherford International shares valued at \$8,000

  - \$2000 cash 

**After Merger:**

Capital Gain = 10,000 -- 9075 = \$**925 (taxable)**.

**Spin-off / Demerger**

A spin-off occurs when a parent company distributes shares of a
subsidiary to its shareholders. Usually non-taxable.

**Example:**\
\
Investor holds 100 shares of Alibaba Group Holding:

- Cost = \$69,500

- Market Value = \$100,000

After spin-off:

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Company**        **Shares**   **Cost      **Market      **Proportion**

  Holding

***Key Takeaways***

- ***Corporate Actions** are decisions by a company's board that affect
  shareholders financially or structurally.*

- *They are classified as **Mandatory**, **Voluntary**, or **Mandatory
  with Choice**.*

- ***Stock Splits** increase the number of shares and lower the price
  per share, but market capitalization remains unchanged.*

- ***Reverse Splits** reduce the number of shares and increase the price
  per share, while market capitalization also remains unchanged.*

- ***Mergers** can be **Taxable** (capital gains/losses recognized)
  or **Non-Taxable** (cost basis carries forward).*

- ***Spin-offs/Demergers** distribute subsidiary shares to existing
  shareholders; usually non-taxable, and total value before and after
  remains approximately equal.*

- ***Cash in Lieu** is provided for fractional shares resulting from
  corporate actions.*
