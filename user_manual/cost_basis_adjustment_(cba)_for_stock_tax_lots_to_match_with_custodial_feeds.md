**Entering CBA for Direct Equity**

This process is used to adjust the original purchase cost without
changing any holding units. The\
system will allow to do Positive / Negative amount adjustments.

**Step 1**

- Go to **Menu **\> **Transactions** \> **Direct Equity**.

- Click on the "+" sign.

![](media/image1.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.033333333333333in"}

**Step 2**

- Select "Type" as "Cost Basis Adjustment (CBA)".

- Select the account for which you want to post the CBA transaction.

- Ledger : Default will automatically be selected as "transfer clearing"
  but you can select any other ledger account.

- Transaction date is the date at which CBA transaction will be posted &
  then select the Security name.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.2041666666666666in"}

**Step 3**

- The system will show whatever open lots there are, in a table with the
  available quantity and the buy price / tax basis.

- Buy price is the adjusted buy price if any other CBA transaction is
  added.

- You can enter details of any of the fields (Buy Price / Tax Basis) and
  the other two fields will be automatically calculated.

- Through this process you can adjust for multiple lots.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.1979166666666665in"}

**Step 4**

- Save the transaction post adjusting the cost.

- You can then see the listing on the details page.

- Additionally, you can go to the voucher and see the subsequent + ve
  / - ve amount as the debited / credited of the custodian account.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.910416666666667in"}

**Report impact of Cost Basis Adjustments:**

- **Wealth Register / Balance Sheet / Gains Report**:\
  From the date of CBA transaction the cost price / purchase amount will
  be adjusted as per the CBA transaction. In case of multiple CBA
  transactions, system will take the last adjusted price.

- **Portfolio Performance:** Will not have any impact of CBA
  adjustments. It will consider the original purchase price.
