**About dashboard settings**

For every new dashboard you add to your report book, you can tweak them
just the way you like using AV's easy settings, which allow you to
customize dashboards individually, or all at once.

Tip:  Want to know more about the report book? Then check out our
article on '[Creating AV report
book](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403260437393-Creating-AV-report-book)'. 

**Customize individual dashboards **

**Step 1 **

You\'ve already added a new dashboard --- by clicking the \'+\' sign in
the \'Report Book\' section of the \'Menu\' --- so now what?
Customization, of course! All you\'ve got to do is fill your details in
each tab of the pop-up dialogue box.

**\'General\' Tab**

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

- **Dashboard Name:** Give your dashboard a name.

- **Auto Refresh To Date:** Change the \'To Date\' filter to either the
  previous day\'s or the previous month-end's date, which will instantly
  auto-process process the report with the new numbers.

Tip: Once you have completed your selection of Auto Refresh, the
dashboard will auto-save your widgets for the next time you view the
dashboard, allowing you to directly generate the PDF.

Notes:

 1. If you've selected 'None' (the default option), then the dashboard
will show your saved cached data. 

2\. If you\'ve selected \'Previous Day\' or \'Previous Month\', all
widgets will auto-refresh as soon as you land on the dashboard which
could mean slightly longer loading times for larger sets of data. 

3\. This feature is available only at dashboard level, and not
individual widget level.

- **Color Scheme: **Set the theme for your charts (bar and pie); the
  \'AV Orange Scheme\' is the default, but you can change this to the
  \'AV Blue Scheme\' too.

- **Grid Configuration:** We\'ve got a default size of 20X32, but you
  can use the other options to expand or contract widget sizes as per
  your needs.

Tip: A higher grid configuration means smoother transitions as you drag
and drop widgets. Learn about this feature in the 'Adding widgets'
section of our '[Creating AV report
book](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403260437393-Creating-AV-report-book)'
article. 

- **Currency format:**Choose the currency format in which you prefer to
  view your data/values.

Note: Your dashboards' currency format is the default chosen for the AV
system, but you can change this using the dropdown list.

- **Decimal Preference:** Specify the number of decimal places you\'d
  want to see for Quantity, Values, Percentages, and Price and FX rates
  from the dropdowns.

- **Widgets Counter:** Users can track the number of widgets added to a
  dashboard -- Essential subscribers can add up to 2 dashboards, and 4
  widgets per dashboard; Pro and Prime users can add up to 20 widgets
  per dashboard while creating unlimited dashboards.

**\'Branding\' Tab**

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

- **Brand Logo: **Upload your logo to see it on the exported PDF.

- **Cover Page Background & Back Page Background: **Upload an image each
  for the first and last page of your exported PDF.

- **Cover Page Notes & Back Page Notes:** You can even add text on the
  cover page/last page of your PDF.

**'Print Preferences' tab**

![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

- Select items as per your print preference to print a PDF file for the
  chosen dashboard.

**Step 2 **

Click the 'Save' icon to save your preferences.

![](media/image6.png){width="6.268055555555556in"

**Additional settings to customize dashboards**

You can also apply certain settings to all your dashboards at once.
These will override the existing settings made to individual dashboards.

**Step 3 **

Edit dashboards from the dashboard settings menu at the top right of
your 'dashboard' section in the Report Book.

![](media/image7.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.3819444444444446in"}

- **Lock/Unlock Dashboard:**You can add, delete, or copy the dashboard
  in \'unlock\' mode, while you\'re blocked from most actions in the
  \'lock\' mode.

Notes: 

1\. You can edit the dashboard while it is locked, but the changes will
not be saved.

 2. Your dashboard automatically locks when you log out or leave the
Report Book; you must click the 'unlock' icon to edit. 

- **Add Widget:** Add a widget to your dashboard.\
  Learn to customize these using the '[*Configuring widgets using widget
  settings*](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/19873000978585-Configuring-widgets-using-widget-settings)'
  article.

- **Add Dashboard:** Click the icon to add another dashboard.

- **Copy Dashboard:** Duplicate your dashboard and its settings.\
  Are you a Multi-family Office? Then use this option to copy your
  dashboards!

- **Remove Dashboard:** Delete a dashboard using this button.

- **Dashboard Filter:**This set of global filters applies to individual
  dashboards.

  - **Entity/Group:** Name the Entity or Group to be added to your
    chosen widget.

  - **Report Period:** Choose between a custom period, the previous
    month or quarter, and more.

  - **Txn From Date and Tnx To Date:** Set a start and end date for your
    dashboard, even for those who\'ve chosen the \'Custom\' period.

  - **Currency:** Select your preferred reporting currency.

  - **Denomination:** Select the denomination that best allows you to
    read large figures.

  - **\'Process\' icon:** Click this to save your changes.

Note: These changes will apply to every widget on the current dashboard
and will override the previous filter selection for all widgets

- 

- **Dashboard Settings:** Apply different settings like Grid Display,
  Widget Swap, and Widget Push to your dashboard to further customize
  how it looks.

- **Print Dashboard:** Get your dashboard onto your device, and in PDF
  format too. Learn more in our article on '[*Creating AV report
  book*](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403260437393-Creating-AV-report-book)'.

- **Save Dashboards:** Save changes made in the current dashboard.

- **Save all Dashboards:** Save changes done for all dashboards.

- **Report Book Group:** Click this icon to access another screen, which
  helps you configure and print the Report Book.

Tip:  Remember to save your dashboard and its settings to be able to
print them with the right information.
