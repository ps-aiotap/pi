**About the Multi-Period Income Statement**

The multi-period income statement is a financial report that summarizes
an entity\'s income and expenses over multiple periods. It is a time
series analysis of profits or losses of any entity in a single screen.

You can select either monthly, quarterly, or yearly periods, and also
the number of such periods from a particular start date, to analyze the
data.

**Finding the Multi-Period Income Statement**

**Step 1 **

Go to the multi-period income statement by going to 'Menu', then
'General Ledger', and finally, 'Multi-Period Income Statement'.

**Generating the Multi-Period Income Statement**

**Setting a Template**

**Step 2 **

Before we dive into generating the multi-period income statement let\'s
set a template for it. Use our predefined templates - US GAAP and IFRS).
Or, you can configure a custom template from the 'Chart of Accounts'. Go
to 'Menu', 'Masters', then to 'Chart of Accounts'.

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

Learn how to set up and customize the Chart of Accounts with [this
article](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018745738-Setting-up-and-customizing-chart-of-accounts-COA-using-the-base-GAAP-and-IFRS-templates).

**Selecting Report filters**

**Step 3 **

Select the appropriate filters to obtain the desired multi-period income
statement:

![](media/image3.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

- **Template:** This dropdown has predefined US GAAP and IFRS templates,
  and will also reflect any custom template defined for the selected
  entity by the user.

- **'Period\' and 'Number of Periods':** You can select monthly,
  quarterly or yearly periods along with the number of periods. In the
  below screenshot, by selecting the filters as shown, you can view
  income statements for 10 monthly periods starting from January 2021.
  AV supports up to 12 monthly periods.

Tip: If you want to run a quarterly income statement, then choose Period
as "Quarterly" and Number of Periods as "4". Additionally, selecting
January in the 'Starting From' field and 2021 as your 'Starting Year'
will populate the multi-period income statement for 4 quarters, starting
from January 2021.
