**About Pie & Table widget**

This powerful widget is designed to offer diverse data visualization
options within the report book. It empowers you to tailor your data
representation via a Table, a Pie chart, or both, for your analytics
report.

**Navigating to the report book**

**Step 1 **

To see your widget, go to 'Report Book' from the 'Menu' and find 'Pie &
Table' in your widget library.

*For detailed information about the report book, please refer to
'[Creating AV report
book](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403260437393-The-AV-Report-Book-explained)* *'.*

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

Note: Can\'t see it? Check your access permissions from the \'User
Access Permissions\' tab in the \'Settings\' section of the \'Menu\'.
Learn more from the \'[*User Access
Permissions*](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018470337-Setting-up-users-and-user-profile-permissions)\'
article.

**Setting up the Pie & Table widget**

**Step 2 **

Once you\'ve dragged and dropped the 'Pie & Table' widget onto your
dashboard, click the filter icon to filter the data on your screen.

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

![](media/image3.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

- **Chart Type:** See your data in either 'Table' or 'Pie' or 'Pie +
  Table' chart format where each has its own strengths and purposes for
  better data visualization.

- **Chart Value:** Based on the report type selected, the chart too will
  be made of selected chart values.\
  Additional Chart Label: This enlists the same values as that of the
  'Chart Value' and can be shown alongside them.

Note: You can select 'None' if you don't want to show additional labels
on the chart.

- **Report Type:** Based on the widgets selected, it enlists all
  applicable report types like \'Wealth Register\', \'Cumulative Multi
  Period Performance\', \'Custom Multi Period Performance\', \'Portfolio
  Performance\', \'Private Equity\'.

- **Other Fields:** You can explore others options like 'Display
  Currency', 'Show Auto Valuations In Italic', 'Include Partnership
  Positions' etc. to configure your data.

**Step 3 **

the widget will appear with data, as shown below.
