periods. Follow these steps to access and use the Cashflow Projection
Widget in the AV Report Book: 

*Note: This article is specifically centric to Fixed Income Instrument
Type Cashflow Projection and only for 'Sources of Cash'*

File
Path: **Menu **\> **Report Book **\> **Widget Library **\> **Cashflow widget**

(User can drag and drop the Cashflow widget onto the respective

**2. Configuring Your Projection:** 

1.  **Select Your Entity/Group -**\
    Choose the legal entity or portfolio for which you want to model
    future cashflows.

2.  **Pick a Table/Chart -** \
    Decide a table or chart based on how you wish to visualize your cash
    flow followed by a series.

3.  **Group Your Data -**\
    Use **Primary** and **Secondary** Grouping (and optional
    sub-groupings) to slice results. For example: - by asset class or
    sector. 

4.  **Set the Projection Period -** \
    **Period Frequency:** Monthly / Quarterly / Yearly \
    **Number of Periods:** How many future intervals to forecast (e.g.,
    12 months, 4 quarters). 

5.  **Anchor Your Start Date -**\
    The "As on Date" locks in your holdings and balances at that point
    in time. 

6.  **Choose Your Currency -**\
    View results in your preferred currency; conversions match those
    used in other AV reports and denomination.

**3. Understanding the Output:** 

- **Interest Payouts** 

<!-- -->

- AV calculates each coupon payment based on your holdings at the "As on
  Date" and the instrument's payout schedule. 

<!-- -->

- Payments appear in the column corresponding to the actual payment
  month, quarter, or year. 

<!-- -->

- **Principal / Maturity Amounts** 

<!-- -->

- For each fixed-income instrument, the redemption or face-value payment
  posts in the period when it matures.

**4. Best Practices:** 

- **Lock Your Data** 

<!-- -->

- Always confirm your "As on Date" to ensure you're projecting from the
  correct balance snapshot. 

<!-- -->

- **Tailor Your Groupings** 

<!-- -->

- Use sub-groupings (e.g., instrument type, credit rating) to highlight
  specific cashflow drivers. 

<!-- -->

- **Check Your Horizon**

<!-- -->

- For long-term forecasts (several years), remember that coupon rates
  and maturity terms are assumed constant.

**5. When to Use Cashflow Projections:** 

- **Liquidity Planning:** Anticipate when large inflows or outflows
  occur. 

<!-- -->

- **Risk Management:** Identify gaps in your cash-in profile vs.
  liabilities. 

<!-- -->

- **Reporting & Audits:** Share clear, period-by-period forecasts with
  stakeholders.
