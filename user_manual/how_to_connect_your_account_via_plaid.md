**Plaid** is a fintech platform that enables secure connections between
user's financial accounts and digital applications. It provides APIs
that let apps access verified banking and credit card data---like
balances, transactions, and account details---without storing sensitive
credentials. Integrating Plaid allows users to easily and safely link
their bank and credit card accounts in just a few clicks, improving
onboarding speed and trust.

For our users, this means a seamless experience when connecting
accounts, real-time financial insights, and greater control over their
data.

 

**Step 1 --- Open the App Store**

- Go to **Menu → App Store** and click on the **Plaid** tile\
  ![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
  height="4.433333333333334in"}

- Click on the **Plaid** tile and Click on **Connect Institution via
  Plaid**\
  ![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
  height="3.1166666666666667in"}

                       

**Step 2 --- Connect With Plaid**

AV will automatically open a secure Plaid pop-up window (Plaid Link).\
![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.2555555555555555in"} 

- Use your **Phone Number** to Log In.

- Use the code sent on your phone number.

**Step 3 --- Choose Your Bank**

- Click on **Add new account** .

**   **![](media/image5.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="4.541666666666667in"}** **

1.  Search for your bank or credit card provider.

2.  Log in using your online banking credentials.

3.  Complete MFA (multi-factor authentication) if asked.

? Your credentials never pass through AV --- Plaid handles them
securely.

** **

**Step 4 --- Finish Connection**

After logging in, you'll see all your available accounts (checking,
savings, credit card, etc.).

           ![](media/image6.png){width="5.625in"
height="7.826388888888889in"} 

Click **Continue** to authorize Plaid.\
\
You will see a list of your connected bank accounts with all the
details\
![](media/image7.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5861111111111112in"}

AV will start fetching your data in the background.

**? What Happens Next**

After you connect your accounts:

- The system begins an **initial sync**, fetching:

  - 12 months of transaction history

  - Account balances

  - Basic account information

- After the first sync, data updates automatically **once per day**.

If you try to process transactions too soon (within 30 minutes of
linking), you'll see this friendly reminder:

⚠️ *"The data sync is still in progress, please try after some time."*
