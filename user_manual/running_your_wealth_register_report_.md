**What is the wealth register?**

AV\'s analytics reporting suite provides key reports to slice and dice
investment positions and group them by a primary and secondary grouping
of your choice. The wealth register is AV\'s flagship holding report for
a total wealth view across a single entity or a group of multiple
entities, with or without partnership look-through, as on a particular
selected report date.

All active holdings as on that date are priced and consolidated within
the report with additional holding and since inception performance
details below:

Allocation%

Quantity

Market Price

Purchase Value

Value on Date

Unrealized Gains

**Finding the wealth register report**

**Step 1**

Go to 'Menu', then 'Analytics', and click 'Wealth Register'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

**How to select appropriate filters**

**Step 2**

In the report filter, select any date to view market values, IRR, and
unrealized gains as of that date.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}

**Step 3**

\'Primary Grouping' and 'Secondary Grouping' help you analyze data to
evaluate your investments based on different parameters like Asset
Class, Pre-set Strategy (sub-asset class), Advisor-wise holdings,
Sectoral breakup, and more.

 

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.4770833333333333in"}

Note: You can add other additional columns (like IRR, Accrued Income and
Position ID) to the report by checking them in the 'Show Columns'
drop-down.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.2284722222222224in"}

**Step 4**

Finally, run your report with various filters to get the desired view of
your holdings.
