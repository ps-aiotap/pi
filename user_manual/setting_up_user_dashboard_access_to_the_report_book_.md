**About this feature**

Special users can also be allowed access to a particular dashboard in
your report book.

*Learn more about creating AV report
book, *[here](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/8104162677533-Creating-configurable-reports)*. *

**Step 1 **

In the 'User' section, click the 'Dashboard Access Permission' icon to
access a pop-up window.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"}

Tip: Want to know how to create and manage users and their permissions?
Check
out [this](https://support.assetvantage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018470337) article. 

**Step 2**

Select an existing dashboard from the \'Add Dashboards\' section.

![](media/image2.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

**Step 3**

Then move this to the \'Selected Dashboard\' section by clicking the
\'Add\' button. Your selected user can now view the chosen dashboard.
Finally, hit 'Save' to save your changes.

![](media/image3.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.452777777777778in"}

**We hope you are successfully able to choose who can see your report
book. Still have questions? Feel free to reach out to AV\'s Customer
Success Team. **
