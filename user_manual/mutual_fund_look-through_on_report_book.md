Ever wondered what\'s really inside your mutual fund? With MF
Look-through, you can see the full breakdown of your mutual fund
holdings. This feature reveals not just the direct investments but also
the indirect ones, providing a complete picture of your portfolio.

**What is MF Look-through?**

MF Look-through is a tool designed to give you an in-depth look at all
the securities your mutual fund invests in. It offers a transparent
view, allowing you to see both direct and indirect exposures to various
securities. This means you can better understand where your money is
going and make more informed investment decisions.

**How to Access the MF Look-through Report**

**Step 1**

Navigating to the Report

1.  Login to your account.

2.  Go to 'Menu'.

3.  Select 'Report Book'.

4.  Click on the 'Look-through widget'.

![](media/image1.gif){width="6.268055555555556in" height="2.91875in"}

*Can\'t see it?* Check your permission status in the 'User Access
Permission' section. For more details, *please refer to the [User access
permission
article](https://assetvantage.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/1070000063060-setting-up-users-and-user-profile-permissions)*.

**Step 2**

Selecting Filters

1.  Choose the appropriate filters:

    - **Table/Chart:** Decide how you want to view your data---Table,
      Bar, or Pie chart.

    - **Investment Universe Filter and Sub Filter:** These allow you to
      filter the data further, though they won\'t be used for grouping.

    - **Count N:** This filter lets you view the top 'N' allocations.
      For example, you can see your top 5 holdings in the report.

![](media/image2.gif){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.9118055555555555in"}

**Step 3 **

Generating the Report

1.  After selecting your filters, click 'Process' to generate your MF
    Look-through report.

*Note:*

- *The system will show the underlying breakups, but the
  Allocation/Valuation will be minimal in underlying MFs/Securities of
  Funds of fund.*

- *This feature is mainly for customers based out of India.*
