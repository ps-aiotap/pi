The AV system allows you to upload transactions or data from contract
note documents directly into the platform. If your contract
note contains data related to Direct Equity, Derivatives, Mutual Funds,
or Fixed Income, follow these steps to extract and automatically upload
transactions into the system. 

**Upload Contract Note** 

**Step 1** 

Go to **Menu \> Transactions \> Direct Equity.** 

**Step 2** 

Click on the Contract Notes tab located at the top right corner of the
screen. 

**Step 3** 

Select Required Fields Before Uploading the Contract Note: 

- Select the Entity, then choose the Delivery Depository/Custodian
  Account, and select the Payee/Payor/Bank in the Direct Equity module. 

<!-- -->

- These fields are mandatory and must be filled in to post the contract
  note into the system. 

***Note:** These fields are mandatory and must be filled in to
successfully post the contract note into the system.) The **Default
PPB** (Payee/Payor/Bank) will be auto-filled by the system.*

*Please refer to the video at the bottom of the article for a visual
representation of how it actually appears in the system.*

**Step 4** 

- You can Drag & Drop or Browse the contract note document into the
  dedicated slot. 

<!-- -->

- If the document is password protected, a pop-up will appear where you
  can enter the password and press OK to upload it. 

<!-- -->

- Once the upload bar is complete, the data will be directly available
  on the upload screen. 

<!-- -->

- If the contract note contains Direct Equity, Derivatives, Mutual Fund,
  or Fixed Income transactions, once uploaded and processed, all Direct
  Equity transactions will appear on the upload screen. 

<!-- -->

- A pop-up will notify you that transactions in Derivatives and other
  asset classes are also present. (\<gifs\>) 

<!-- -->

- Press 'OK' in the pop-up, and you will be redirected to a different
  tab. After a few seconds, the Derivative upload screen will populate
  with your extracted derivative transactions. 

**Step 5** 

- If you are in the Contract Note tab or any other redirected tab, you
  can review your extracted transactions, edit them if needed, and then
  mark the checkboxes before clicking on Process to post the
  transactions into the system. 

<!-- -->

- You can confirm the processing by switching to the Details tab, where
  you will see your processed transactions.

<!-- -->

- On the **Contract Note Upload** screen, click the **edit icon** on the
  left side of any transaction. This will open the **Net Obligation
  Pop-up Screen**. 

<!-- -->

- If your uploaded contract note has transactions from different modules
  like **Equity**, **Derivatives**, **Mutual Funds**, or **Fixed
  Income**, you will be able to **view and edit** these transactions by
  switching through the **tabs with the respective module names**. 

<!-- -->

- After making the necessary changes, click on the **Save icon** to
  apply the updates. Once saved, the pop-up will automatically close. 

<!-- -->

- you can **check the boxes** and **process the desired transaction** by
  hitting the **Process icon**. 

***Note:** Don't forget to save your changes before closing the pop-up
to ensure all updates are applied successfully.* 

*Please refer to the video at the bottom of the article for a visual
representation of how it actually appears in the system.*

**Step 6** 

- Be cautious when uploading multiple contract notes back to back and
  posting their transactions. 

<!-- -->

- For example, if you upload a contract note and the upload screen
  displays your extracted transactions, but you don't process all the
  items, some transactions might remain on the screen. This can happen
  due to errors or if you choose not to process certain transactions. 

<!-- -->

- In such cases, it's recommended to **clear the remaining
  transactions** by clicking on the **Reset button** to remove any
  previously uploaded data. 

<!-- -->

- If you don't clear the screen, the **previously uploaded data** will
  appear during your next upload, which can lead to confusion.
