Capital flow by Investments report helps to understand and track
Contributions, Withdrawals, Transfers, Income/Expense, Net Gain/loss and
Change in value by positions along with Opening/Closing valuations. You
can also track your performance with Period IRR%, Period BM
IRR%, Inception IRR%, Inception BM IRR%, Period TWR%, Period BM
TWR%, Inception TWR% and Inception BM TWR% on CFI report.\
Capital flow by investments report is the capital flow report configured
by investment positions using Primary and Secondary groupings.

Capital flow by investments report will be available to generate by
standard AV grouping filters i.e. by Asset class/Strategy;
Advisor/Account; Account/Strategy etc. along with Partnership support.

**Finding Capital Flow by Investment report**

- To generate this report, go to \'Menu\', head to \'Analytics\', and
  select 'Capital Flow by Investments\'.

       Path: Menu \> Analytics \> Capital Flow by Investments

**Generating CFI on the report book **

- Go to 'Menu', 'Report Book', then to 'Add Widget', and finally, to the
  'Capital Flow' widget.

![](media/image4.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="3.5256944444444445in"} 

- Click the filter icon to get a pop-up that lets you select your report
  type. There, choose 'Capital Flow Investment\' check 'Benchmark' in
  the 'Other fields' section if required, and click 'Process' to
  generate the report.
