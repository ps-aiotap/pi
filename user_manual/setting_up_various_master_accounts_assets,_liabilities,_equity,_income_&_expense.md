**Accounts : Manual Entry**

The "Master" module is a one stop point for creating and maintaining
universal accounts within your system which will be refenced, tagged and
used throughout your system while uploading transactions and viewing
analytical reports.

- Go to **Menu **\> **Masters **\> **Accounts **\> "**+**" Sign to
  Manually Add accounts. 

- As discussed in the beginning of this handbook, you can either
  manually add or directly upload in bulk.

- The "Class : Account Type" defines the various Account types which can
  be selected in the AV system. They are namely:

  1.  **Asset: Custodian - **They are responsible for holding onto your
      investments, such as stocks, bonds, and mutual funds. Think of
      them as the safe keeper for your investments, just like a bank
      keeps your money safe.

  2.  **Asset: Bank -** This represents your everyday money for
      depositing and withdrawing. It\'s like your financial center for
      managing your cash flow. For example, Bank accounts.

  3.  **Asset: Ledger -** This keeps track of the money customers owe
      you for goods or services they\'ve received but haven\'t paid for
      yet. It\'s like a record of pending payments, including things
      like inventory and accounts receivable.

  4.  **Asset: Payor -** This is an intermediatory account used to
      monitor funds not received for a sold asset through a third party.
      For instance, if you have sold land through a broker, the broker
      serves as the payor, facilitating the transaction.

  5.  **Liability: Bank - **This includes money owed to the bank in the
      form of loans, credit card debt, or overdraft facilities. These
      are financial obligations that need to be repaid by you.

  6.  **Liability: Loan -** This keeps track of any loans you\'ve taken
      from various sources, whether it\'s a personal loan, business
      loan, or other types of borrowing. It\'s a record of the money you
      owe that needs to be repaid.

  7.  **Liability: Ledger - **This tracks money you owe to suppliers or
      vendors for goods or services received on credit. For example,
      inventory amount payable, stock purchased on credit etc.

  8.  **Liability: Payee - **This is an intermediatory account used to
      monitor funds unpaid for a purchased asset through a third party.
      For instance, if you have purchased land through a broker, the
      broker serves as the payee, facilitating the transaction.

  9.  **Income: Income Account - **These track various sources of income
      for you or your business (dividends, interest, salary, rent).

  10. **Expense: Expense Account -** These track various expenses
      incurred for you or your business (STT, brokerage, salaries
      payable).

  11. **Equity: Ledger - **This tracks the initial investment made by
      the owner(s) in the business. This represents the owner\'s
      financial stake in the business.

**Asset : Custodian**

**Steps to Create a Custodian Account:**

1.  **Select Account Type:**

    - In the \"Class: Account Type\" field, choose \"Asset: Custodian\".

2.  **Entity and Account Details:**

    - Select the entity for which you want to create the account.

    - Enter the account name and number, along with the currency used.

3.  **Account Purpose:**

    - In the \"For\" field, specify the account\'s purpose (e.g., AMC
      account, brokerage account).

4.  **Short Code (Optional):**

    - Assign a custom short code for easy transaction identification.

5.  **Display Name:**

    - The system automatically generates a clear display name combining
      all entered details.

6.  **Capitalize Expenses (Optional):**

    - This option capitalizes specific transaction charges
      (brokerage, stamp duty, etc.) for Direct Equity and Mutual Funds
      transactions uploaded in bulk.

7.  **Default Payee/Payor:**

    - Select a default payee/payor account (explained further on) for
      this custodian account.

8.  **Additional Information (Optional): **You can enter details like:

- Second/Third Holder(s)

- Nominee

- Tax Code and Section

- Payment Date (if applicable)

- Linked Accounts (for US customers only)

- Address

**Asset : Bank**

1.  **Select Account Type:**

**Accounts : Bulk Upload**

![](media/image10.png){width="6.268055555555556in"
height="2.252083333333333in"}

1.  **Navigate to Uploads:**

    - Access the \"Menu\" section, located at the top right corner

    - Look for a tab or option labeled \"Masters\" within that
      \"Account\"

2.  **Download the Account Template:**

    - Within the Uploads section, identify a function to download a
      template file. This file is named \"AV format\" or similar,
      indicating its use for account uploads.

3.  **Fill in Account Details:**

    - Open the downloaded spreadsheet using Excel.

    - The spreadsheet will have pre-defined columns corresponding to
      various account details. You\'ll need to enter information for
      each account you want to upload. Here\'s a general breakdown of
      the columns you might encounter (refer to your specific
      platform\'s documentation for exact details):

      - **Class: Account Type:** Select the appropriate type from a
        dropdown menu or pre-defined options (e.g., Asset: Payor,
        Liability: Ledger, etc.).

      - **Entity:** Specify the entity for which the account belongs.

      - **Account Name & Number:** Enter a descriptive name and unique
        account number for each account.

      - **For:** Provide an optional description of the account\'s
        purpose (e.g., \"Rent Receivable\", \"Salary Account\").

      - **Short Code:** Assign an optional custom short code for easier
        transaction identification.

      - **Opening Balance:** Enter the initial balance for each account
        if you want to track transactions before the book start date.

      - **Tax Code & Section, Payment Date, Address:** Fill in these
        fields as applicable based on the account type and your local
        accounting regulations.

4.  **Save the Spreadsheet:**

    - After entering all account details, save the completed spreadsheet
      file.

5.  **Upload the File:**

    - Return to the Uploads section.

    - Locate the \"Upload\" button or functionality and select the saved
      spreadsheet file containing your account data.

    - The platform will process the upload, and you should receive a
      confirmation message or notification upon successful upload.

- **Reviewing Uploaded Accounts:**

  1.  **Review Screen:**

      - After uploading your account data, the system will display a
        list of all uploaded accounts.

  2.  **Identify Errors:**

      - Entries will be color-coded for easy identification:

        - **Green Rows:** These entries were uploaded successfully with
          no errors.

        - **Pink Rows:** These entries contain errors and require
          correction.

  3.  **Fix Errors:**

      - For entries with errors (pink rows), you can directly fix them
        by clicking the \"Edit\" button next to the specific entry.

      - An \"Account Add/Edit\" dialog box will appear, allowing you to
        modify the details.

  4.  **Check Mandatory Fields:**

      - The system will likely highlight mandatory fields (usually with
        yellow horizontal lines) to ensure they are filled in correctly
        and no essential information is missing.

      - Verify and correct any missing or incorrect data in the
        mandatory fields.

  5.  **Process Upload:**

      - Once all errors are fixed and all rows are green, signifying a
        successful review, select all entries using the checkbox option
        (usually a square box next to each entry).

      - Click the \"Process\" button to finalize the upload process.

  6.  **View Uploaded Entries (Optional):**

      - The system might offer a \"Details\" tab beside the \"Uploads\"
        tab where you can view all uploaded account details after
        successful processing.
